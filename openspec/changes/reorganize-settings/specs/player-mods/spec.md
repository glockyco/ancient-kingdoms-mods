## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Bestiary pages reveal every monster

While the General setting Undiscovered monsters is on, as at first start, Pocket Compendium SHALL show a monster's full Bestiary page whether or not the player has discovered or killed it: name, level, lore, health, armor, resists, type, class, zone and loot. A loot slot SHALL show the item in full color, and hovering it SHALL show the item's complete tooltip with its stats, whether or not the item has dropped for the player.

#### Scenario: Undiscovered boss

- **WHEN** a player who has never discovered Xylevis opens its Bestiary page and hovers a loot slot
- **THEN** the page shows Xylevis's stats and lore, and the tooltip lists the item's slot, stats, durability, level requirement and price
