## Why

The `reader-facing-copy` spec mixed rules a page can be checked against with voice rules. The voice rules made text stiffer, as Bloodline's research found, and one forbade the "you" of the approved README. Reader text is now chosen in comparisons that start from how large game wikis word the same thing (`.local/changes/rewrite-reader-facing-copy/review/round-1/`).

## What Changes

- Remove six voice requirements.
- Add a requirement that reader text is chosen in a comparison, with its facts checked against the game.
- Keep the checkable requirements as they are. Skill summaries keep "dmg" and "max hp", and no check rejects semicolons.
