## Why

Product decisions kept only in the personal vault are unavailable to repository contributors and drift from the requirements. Record the decisions that still govern the compendium beside its existing contracts before removing the duplicate vault entries.

## What Changes

- Extend reader-facing copy requirements with the established terminology, explanation and changelog rules.
- Record website presentation boundaries, retained layouts and page naming without changing the interface.
- Preserve the content exceptions that general redaction rules do not describe.
- Record the remaining mod presentation and packaging contracts.
- Document shared runtime coordination, scratch-character handling and release checks in the contributor workflow.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `website-presentation`: established page composition, navigation names and retained interaction boundaries.

### Modified Capabilities

- `reader-facing-copy`: established writing and changelog rules.
- `compendium-redaction`: published recipe-note and prefab-NPC exceptions.
- `player-mods`: loader packaging, tooltip presentation and augment feature imagery.
- `game-calculators`: calculation inputs stay local and avoid optimization rankings.
- `site-deployment`: cache invalidation policy.

## Impact

Specifications and contributor documentation only. No new reader-facing wording or design is selected, no code changes, and no deployment.
