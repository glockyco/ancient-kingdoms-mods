# Proposal

## Why

The Skills panel beside a Bestiary page is the one part of Pocket Compendium that still looks unfinished: a flat dark box, 660 px wide and as tall as the Journal, whose table rows fill the top third. Each row gives damage as a formula such as "attack power + 5 physical dmg", which a player cannot turn into a number, while the website shows the number at the monster's level. The rows also leave out what players prepare a fight around: how far an area attack reaches, which attacks land without a cast bar, which debuffs no cleanse removes, and when a monster heals or enrages.

## What Changes

- The panel becomes a window framed like the game's own, docked beside the Bestiary page at its height. It opens with the page and stays closed once the player closes it.
- A summary comes first: the damage the monster deals by type, with the largest amount of each type, and the attacks and effects to prepare for.
- Each special skill gets a card with its name, shape and reach in yd, damage at the monster's level by type, effects with chance and duration, cooldown, cast time, and whether a cast bar announces it. The basic attack and each passive take one line.
- Hovering a card shows the skill's full mechanics in the website's terms.
- Damage amounts come from the monster's own stats, computed as on the website.
- The panel and the in-fight skill cards show special skills with the same names, icons and order.
- The layout, the summary and every label are chosen through comparisons with screenshots, and every behaviour the panel states is observed in the running game before it ships.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `bestiary-skill-panel`: what the Skills panel beside a Bestiary page shows, where it sits, and how it matches the in-fight skill cards.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Code: `mods/PocketCompendium/Features/BestiaryPages/Ui/SkillsPanel*`, the effect extractor and formatter in `Features/BestiaryPages/Skills/`, and the skill ordering shared with `Features/BossSkills`.
- Tests: the formatter's parity corpus gains cases with a monster context, generated by `website/scripts/gen-skill-effect-parity.ts`, and the C# parity test reads them.
- Website: no change, unless the parity work finds a defect in the site's own numbers.
- Documentation: the Bestiary section of `mods/PocketCompendium/README.md` and its screenshot.
