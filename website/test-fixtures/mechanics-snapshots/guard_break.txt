Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Armor Class gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Monsters marked Immune to Debuffs ignore this skill. Block Chance Lowers the affected character's Block Chance by 10 percentage points at rank 10, to at least 0%. Block Chance applies only to physical hits. Block Chance is capped at 80% before Accuracy and level difference are applied. The final chance for an attack to miss is capped at 90%.
