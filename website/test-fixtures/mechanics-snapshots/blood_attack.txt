Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Spell Power at its level. Skill damage 500 Damage before defenses Spell Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Spell Power 700 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 500 = 700 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals the attacker for 100% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 200. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap healing bonuses, such as Lich Shroud's, multiply the healing. See lifetap.
