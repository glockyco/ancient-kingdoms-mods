Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has 50% Cleanse Resist and three layers. Cleansing removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each of those rolls succeeds at 50% × (100% + the cleansing caster's Accuracy), limited to 0–100%. At 100%, one cast removes all three layers. See cleanse calculations. After a cleanse removes layers, each damage tick deals 85% with two layers left and 70% with one, rounded up. Block Chance Lowers the affected character's Block Chance by 20 percentage points at rank 1, to at least 0%. Block Chance applies only to physical hits. Block Chance is capped at 80% before Accuracy and level difference are applied. The final chance for an attack to miss is capped at 90%. Accuracy Lowers the affected character's Accuracy by 20 percentage points at rank 1. The chance that its attacks and debuffs are blocked or resisted rises by the same number of percentage points, within each roll's limits. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
