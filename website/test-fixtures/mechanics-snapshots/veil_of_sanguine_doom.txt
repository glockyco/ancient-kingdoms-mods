Mechanics Debuff Scaling Monster or NPC This debuff receives no attribute bonus. The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has three layers, and cleansing it removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each extra-layer roll starts at 50%. Accuracy increases that chance. See cleanse calculations. While layers remain, each damage tick deals 85% of its full damage at two layers or 70% at one layer. Block Chance This skill adds its Block Chance bonus to the target's chance to block. Block Chance is capped at 80% before Accuracy and level difference are applied. The final chance for an attack to miss is capped at 90%. Accuracy This skill's Accuracy bonus lowers the target's chance to block or resist an attack by the same number of percentage points. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
