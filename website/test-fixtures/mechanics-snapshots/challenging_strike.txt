Mechanics Skill Damage Warrior Damage is the value in your character window. Skill damage 25 at rank 1, +25 per rank (125 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Damage Use your numbers 325 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 25 + 25 × (5 − 1) = 125 Damage before defenses: 200 + 125 = 325 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
