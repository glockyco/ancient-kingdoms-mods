Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Attack Power at its level. Skill damage 100 Damage before defenses Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Attack Power 300 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 100 = 300 The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns.
