Each renewal lasts 5s. A recipient loses the effect when it leaves range. When combat starts or ends, the previous set stops renewing and lasts its remaining duration. The Bard mercenary sings automatically. Out of combat, it keeps only Mercenary Wayfarer's Rhythm active. In combat, it keeps Mercenary Grand Symphony and Mercenary March of Celerity active. From level 40, it also keeps Mercenary Anthem of Focus active. It starts one song at a time. All song effects end immediately when the mercenary or its owner dies, or when the mercenary is stunned, feared, or asleep. Active Song Limit The mercenary sustains two combat songs below level 40 and three from level 40 onward (the Active Songs value in the mercenary equipment window). Polyphony and learned song-duration bonuses do not apply to the mercenary. See Bard Songs. Song Category: Bard Focus Song Songs in one category compare strength: the sum of their movement speed, physical damage, magic damage, Haste, spell haste and Accuracy bonuses. Each bonus except movement speed counts after the singer's Charisma scaling. A target keeps one song of each category, including songs from other Bards and Bard mercenaries. A stronger song replaces a song in the same category. At equal strength, the song already active stays. See Bard Songs. Accuracy Raises the affected character's Accuracy by 15.5 percentage points at rank 30, before Charisma scaling. The chance that its attacks and debuffs are blocked or resisted falls by the same number of percentage points, within each roll's limits. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
