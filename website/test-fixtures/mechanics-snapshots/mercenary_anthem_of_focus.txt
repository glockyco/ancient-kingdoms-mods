The mercenary refreshes its aura every 2.5 seconds while the song stays active. The Bard mercenary sings automatically. Out of combat, it keeps only Wayfarer's Rhythm active. In combat, it keeps Grand Symphony and March of Celerity active. From level 40, it also keeps Anthem of Focus active. It starts one song at a time. The songs stop when the mercenary or its owner dies, or when the mercenary is hidden, stunned, feared, or asleep. Active Song Limit The mercenary sustains two combat songs below level 40 and three from level 40 onward. Polyphony and learned song duration bonuses do not apply to the mercenary. Song Category: Bard Focus Song Song strength adds its movement speed, physical damage percentage, magic damage percentage, Haste, spell haste and Accuracy bonuses. A target keeps one song of each category, including songs from other Bards and Bard mercenaries. A stronger song replaces a song in the same category. At equal strength, the song already active stays.
