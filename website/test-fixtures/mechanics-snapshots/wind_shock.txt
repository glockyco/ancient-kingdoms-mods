Mechanics Skill Damage Druid Spell Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 10 at rank 1, +10 per rank (50 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Spell Power + Skill damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers 250 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 10 + 10 × (5 − 1) = 50 Damage before defenses: 200 + 50 = 250 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
