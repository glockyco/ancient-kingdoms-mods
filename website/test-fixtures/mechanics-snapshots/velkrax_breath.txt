Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Spell Power at its level. Skill damage 1,500 Damage before defenses Spell Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Spell Power 1,700 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 1,500 = 1,700 The target's Cold Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Break Armor Each hit that is not blocked, missed or resisted has a 50% chance to trigger Break Armor. When it triggers, the game picks one random equipment slot on the target. If that slot contains an item with durability remaining, the item loses 1–4 durability. Break Armor affects players and mercenaries.
