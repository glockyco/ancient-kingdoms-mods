See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Break Armor Each hit can trigger Break Armor at the skill's listed chance. When it triggers, the game picks one random equipment slot on the target. If that slot contains an item with durability remaining, the item loses 1–4 durability. Break Armor affects players and mercenaries.
