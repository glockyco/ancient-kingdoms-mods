Mechanics Song Aura Song duration = base duration × (1 + learned song duration bonuses) Active Song Limit Active song limit = 2 + the bonus from Polyphony. A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Song Scaling Song multiplier = 1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2) final value = round(base value at skill level × song multiplier) Intelligence does not change this song. Resist Chance clamp( target's Magic Resist × 0.0005 + clamp((target level − skill user level) × 0.005, −0.1, 0.1) − skill user's Accuracy , 0, 0.9) Every 100 points of the target's matching Resist add 5 percentage points to resist chance before level difference and Accuracy. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. This skill bypasses monsters' immunity to debuffs. It lowers the target's resist chance by 30 percentage points before the resist roll. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
