Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Accuracy Raises the affected character's Accuracy by 15 percentage points at rank 10. The chance that its attacks and debuffs are blocked or resisted falls by the same number of percentage points, within each roll's limits. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
