Mechanics Skill Damage Cleric Spell Power and Attack Power are the values in your character window. Skill damage 120 Damage before defenses Spell Power + Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,200 Spell Power·,200 Attack Power Use your numbers 520 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 200 + 120 = 520 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
