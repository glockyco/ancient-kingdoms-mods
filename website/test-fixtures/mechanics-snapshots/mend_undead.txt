Mechanics Companion Buff Targets your living companion within 8 yd. Familiars and mercenaries cannot receive this buff. Buff Scaling Attribute contribution = INT Healing per second skill value at rank × (1 + min(attribute contribution × 0.004, 5.0)) Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 1.2s cast into 0.96s.
