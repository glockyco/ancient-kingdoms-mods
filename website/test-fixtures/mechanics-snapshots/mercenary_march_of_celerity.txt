Mechanics Bard mercenary skill level = min(max skill level, max(1, floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10))). Mercenary Song Aura activeDuration = baseDuration; reapplied every 2.5s while the song stays active The Bard mercenary sings by itself. Out of combat it keeps only Wayfarer's Rhythm active. In combat it keeps Grand Symphony and March of Celerity active, and from level 40 also Anthem of Focus. It starts one song at a time. The songs stop when the mercenary or its owner dies, or when the mercenary is hidden, stunned, feared, or asleep. Active Song Limit maximumCombatSongs = 2 below level 40, 3 at level 40 and above Polyphony and learned song duration bonuses do not apply to the mercenary. Effective Cast Time = castTime − (castTime × spellHaste)
