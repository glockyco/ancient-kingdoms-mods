Mechanics When used by a mercenary, skill level = floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10), capped at max skill level. Buff Scaling Attribute contribution = WIS (mercenary's own attribute) Ward Ward at skill level + attribute contribution × 2 Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 1.25s cast into 1s.
