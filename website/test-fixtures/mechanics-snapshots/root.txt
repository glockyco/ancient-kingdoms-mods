Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Monsters marked Immune to Debuffs ignore this skill. Root Root stops movement and does not break when the target takes damage. Every 2 seconds, a rooted monster rolls to break free. Each point of its Magic Resist adds 0.1 percentage points to that chance, which stays between 5% and 95%. A rooted NPC has a 10% chance to break free every second. Bosses and elite monsters always resist this debuff. A rooted monster still attacks and casts at targets within its reach. Heal on Hit The affected target heals anyone who hits it in melee for 10% of each hit's damage after defenses and critical hits, before the target's Ward absorbs any of it, rounded, up to the attacker's missing Health. A 200-damage hit heals 20 at rank 8. Rank 1 gives 3%, and each rank adds 1%. A melee hit is a non-spell damage skill with a base cast range below 2 yd. Several such effects each round their healing, then add together. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2.
