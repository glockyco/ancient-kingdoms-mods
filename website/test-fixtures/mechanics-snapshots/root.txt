Mechanics The target resists this debuff using Magic Resist. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Root Root stops movement and does not break when the target takes damage. Every 2 seconds, a rooted monster rolls to break free. Each point of its Magic Resist adds 0.1 percentage points to that chance, which stays between 5% and 95%. A rooted NPC has a 10% chance to break free every second. Bosses and elite monsters always resist this debuff. Heal on Hit The affected target heals anyone who hits it in melee for 3% of each hit's damage after defenses and critical hits, rounded, up to the attacker's missing Health. A 200-damage hit heals 6 at rank 1. Each rank adds 1%, reaching 10% at rank 8. A melee hit is a non-spell damage skill with a base cast range below 2 yd. Several such effects each round their healing, then add together.
