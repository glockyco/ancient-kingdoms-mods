Mechanics Lifetap Healing Bonus Adds 15% + 5% × skill lvl to the lifetap healing multiplier. Lifetap damage is unchanged. Bonuses from unexpired and permanent buffs add together. Healing = floor(lifetap percentage × damage after wards and Mana Shield × (1 + summed bonuses)). Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 1.2s cast into 0.96s.
