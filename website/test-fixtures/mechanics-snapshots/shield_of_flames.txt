Mechanics The target's Fire Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Damage Shield Damage Shield reflects damage when a non-spell, single-target melee attack with a cast range below 1.5 hits the wearer. Scroll skills and weapon effects triggered on hit cannot trigger it. The reflected amount uses the caster's attributes at the moment the buff was applied, as Buff Scaling above shows. The attacker's Defense or matching resist reduces the reflected hit. See resistance and mitigation.
