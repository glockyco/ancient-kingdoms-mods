Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Monsters marked Immune to Debuffs ignore this skill. Sleep Sleep stops movement and action, cancels a monster's cast and makes it drop its target. Direct damage, ordinary damage-over-time ticks and applying a damage-over-time debuff wake the target. Ordering your pet to attack a sleeping monster or NPC also wakes it. Reaper Form Health drain does not. Bosses and elite monsters always resist sleep. Every 6 seconds, a sleeping monster, NPC or pet rolls to wake. Each point of its Magic Resist adds 0.1 percentage points to that chance, which stays between 5% and 95%.
