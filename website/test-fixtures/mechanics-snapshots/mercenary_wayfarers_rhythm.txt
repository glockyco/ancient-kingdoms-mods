Mechanics Bard mercenary skill level = min(max skill level, max(1, floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10))). Mercenary Song Aura Song duration = base duration. The mercenary refreshes the song every 2.5s while it stays active. The Bard mercenary sings automatically. Out of combat, it keeps only Wayfarer's Rhythm active. In combat, it keeps Grand Symphony and March of Celerity active. From level 40, it also keeps Anthem of Focus active. It starts one song at a time. The songs stop when the mercenary or its owner dies, or when the mercenary is hidden, stunned, feared, or asleep. Active Song Limit Combat song limit = 2 below level 40, 3 from level 40 onward. Polyphony and learned song duration bonuses do not apply to the mercenary. Song Category: Bard Travel Song Song strength = speed + physical damage % + magic damage % + haste + spell haste + accuracy A target keeps one song of each category, including songs from other Bards and Bard mercenaries. A stronger song replaces a song in the same category. At equal strength, the song already active stays. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
