Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Once it lands, the target's Magic Resist reduces each tick by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. Cleanse layers and other tick modifiers also change the damage that lands. See damage-over-time calculations. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Root Root stops movement and does not break when the target takes damage. A rooted companion or mercenary has a 10% chance to break free every second. A rooted player has no automatic break-free roll.
