Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 total veteran points (your VT), capped at the skill's maximum rank. Resist Chance The target's Disease Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Once it lands, the target's Disease Resist reduces each tick by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. Cleanse layers and other tick modifiers also change the damage that lands. See damage-over-time calculations. This debuff has no effect on monsters whose pages list Immune to Debuffs.
