Mechanics Song Aura The song you select keeps recasting itself. Each cast takes 3.5s, shortened by Spell Casting Haste. Each completed cast renews the song for 20s. After you select another song, this one lasts the rest of its duration. A learned song-duration bonus such as Lingering Melody lengthens it, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 24s. Active Song Limit A Bard sustains two songs by default (the Active Songs value in your character window). Starting another song at the limit ends the song you renewed longest ago. Learning Polyphony removes the limit. See Bard Songs. Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. This debuff has no effect on monsters whose pages list Immune to Debuffs. Accuracy Lowers the affected character's Accuracy by 18 percentage points at rank 6, before Charisma scaling. The chance that its attacks and debuffs are blocked or resisted rises by the same number of percentage points, within each roll's limits. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%. Critical Strike Lowers the affected character's Critical Strike by 6 percentage points at rank 6, before Charisma scaling, to at least 0%. See critical hits for the extra damage.
