Mechanics Song Aura Song duration = base duration × (1 + learned song duration bonuses) Active Song Limit Active song limit = 2 + the bonus from Polyphony. A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Song Scaling Song multiplier = 1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2) final value = base value at skill level × song multiplier Resist Chance clamp( target's Magic Resist × 0.0005 + clamp((target level − skill user level) × 0.005, −0.1, 0.1) − skill user's Accuracy , 0, 0.9) Every 100 points of the target's matching Resist add 5 percentage points to resist chance before level difference and Accuracy. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Accuracy This skill's Accuracy bonus lowers the target's chance to block or resist an attack by the same number of percentage points. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%. Critical Chance This skill adds to the attacker's critical hit chance, capped at 70%. Critical hits deal 1.5 times the damage of a noncritical hit.
