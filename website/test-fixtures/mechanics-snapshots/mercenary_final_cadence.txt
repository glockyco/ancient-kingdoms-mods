Mechanics Bard mercenary skill level = min(max skill level, max(1, floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10))). Final Cadence Requires mercenary level >= 50 and active songs = combat song limit. Base healing = round(skill healing at level × (1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2))) Critical multiplier = 1 normally, 2 on a critical heal, or 3 on 10% of critical heals Final healing = base healing × critical multiplier CHA is the mercenary's Charisma. The mercenary's critical chance determines one roll shared by every recipient. Damage uses the same Charisma multiplier before the usual magic damage reductions. Final Cadence does not heal familiars. The mercenary damages only enemies that target its owner, a party member, or one of their pets. Damage Formula round(skill damage at this level × (1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2))) How Damage Is Calculated Variance: ×0.9–1.1 Backstab: +10% damage when attacking from behind (Rogues with Improved Backstab: +25% instead) Level difference: ±2% per level (max ±20%) Slayer reduction (boss or elite attacking a player or pet): −Slayer level × 10% Enrage: non-spell attacks by monsters below 10% health deal 50–75% more damage. Damage reduction: −ceil(damage × clamp(target's Magic Resist × 0.0005, 0, 0.9)) Critical hit: ×1.5 Radiant Aether (15% on a critical hit, consumes one item): ×2 again, for ×3 total Every 100 points of the matching Resist reduce the damage you take from a hit by 5%, up to a 90% reduction before rounding. Resist Chance clamp( target's Magic Resist × 0.0005 + (target level − attacker level) × 0.005 − attacker's Accuracy , 0, 0.9) Every 100 points of the matching Resist add 5 percentage points to resist chance before Accuracy and level difference. Moving target: resist chance −25 percentage points, damage +10% Backstab: resist chance × 0.8 Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
