A game defect counts a veteran skill rank that costs 2 points as 1 point. Each such rank lowers your Veteran Level by 1 and the next point's experience cost by 20,000 XP, and a token reset refunds only 1 of its 2 points. The mercenary's critical chance determines one roll shared by every recipient. Its damage hits living, visible, attackable monsters within range in the same zone that have a nonempty aggro list and speed above −50. Final Cadence does not heal familiars. The mercenary damages only enemies that target its owner, a party member, or one of their pets. The target's Magic Resist reduces the damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist it entirely.
