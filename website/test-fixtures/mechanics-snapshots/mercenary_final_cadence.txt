Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Use the rank from Mercenary Skill Rank on this page. The examples use the same owner level and veteran points. Critical heals restore double Health, or triple Health in 10% of cases. One critical roll, at the mercenary's critical chance, applies to the healing of every ally. Its damage hits living, visible, attackable monsters within range in the same zone that are already in combat and are not asleep. Final Cadence does not heal familiars. The mercenary damages only enemies that target its owner, any party member, or one of their companions or mercenaries. For each ally healed, healing adds threat equal to a sixth of the Health restored, rounded up, on monsters within 16 yd of the caster that are fighting that ally. The target's Magic Resist reduces the damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist it entirely.
