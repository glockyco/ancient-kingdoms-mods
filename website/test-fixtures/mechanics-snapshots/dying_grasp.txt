Mechanics Skill Damage Necromancer Spell Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 400 Combined damage Spell Power + Skill damage Damage % 200% Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers 1,200 damage before the target's defenses Combined damage: 200 + 400 = 600 Damage before defenses: 600 × 200% = 1,200, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals the attacker for 100% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 200. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap healing bonuses, such as Lich Shroud's, multiply the healing. See lifetap.
