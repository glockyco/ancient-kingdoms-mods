Mechanics Skill Damage Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Necromancer Skill damage 400 Combined damage Magic Damage + Skill damage Rank multiplier 200% Damage before defenses Combined damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 1·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 1,200 damage before the target's defenses Combined damage: 200 + 400 = 600 Damage before defenses: 600 × 200% = 1,200, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals you for 100% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 200. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap Healing bonuses multiply the healing. See lifetap.
