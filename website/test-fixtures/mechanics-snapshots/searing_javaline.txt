Mechanics Skill Damage Cleric Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Skill damage 150 at rank 1, +50 per rank (350 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 550 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 150 + 50 × (5 − 1) = 350 Damage before defenses: 200 + 350 = 550 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
