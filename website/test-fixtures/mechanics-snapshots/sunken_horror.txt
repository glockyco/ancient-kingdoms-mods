Mechanics Aggro Aggro = min(target health, skill aggro + caster maximum health + damage + round(stunChance × stunTime × 10) + round(fearChance × fearTime × 10) ) Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Fear Fear applies only when the skill's fear roll succeeds and the target fails a separate fear resist roll. Fear lasts a random duration between half and all of the skill's fear duration.
