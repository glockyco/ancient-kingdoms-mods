Mechanics Fear Bosses and fear-immune targets cannot be feared. A hit rolls against the fear chance only when it does not stun: the skill has no stun chance, its stun roll fails, or the target is already feared. If the fear roll succeeds, the target rolls its Fear Resist and is feared only if that roll fails. Fear lasts a random time between half and all of the skill's fear duration. A monster or NPC feared by a Necromancer player moves at half its normal speed while feared.
