The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Stun applies on one roll, but cannot affect a feared target. Another stun extends the existing stun instead of replacing its end time. Bear mounts resist 90% of stun attempts. Knockback Knockback applies only if stun and fear did not apply on the same hit and the target is not already stunned. It pushes the target back1.5m and stuns them for 0.25 seconds. If an obstacle blocks the path, the target also takes 1.25% of the damage just dealt, rounded up to at least 1. The target must be within 15 levels of the attacker, unless the attacker is a boss.
