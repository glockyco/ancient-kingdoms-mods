Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 total veteran points (your VT), capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Attack Power at its level. Skill damage 10 at rank 1, +2 per rank (68 at rank 30) Combined damage Attack Power + Skill damage Damage % 100% at rank 1, +0.75% per rank (121.75% at rank 30) Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,the caster's 200 Attack Power·,rank 1 210 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 10 + 2 × (1 − 1) = 10 Combined damage: 200 + 10 = 210 Damage %: 100% + 0.75% × (1 − 1) = 100% Damage before defenses: 210 × 100% = 210, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
