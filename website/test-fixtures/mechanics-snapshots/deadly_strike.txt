Mechanics Skill Damage Rogue Damage is the value in your character window. Enter Base Damage from the weapon's tooltip, even if the weapon is broken. Enter 0 if no weapon is equipped in the named slot. Dexterity contribution 250% of Dexterity above 0, rounded Off-hand deduction Off-hand weapon's Base Damage × 50%, rounded up Skill damage 60 at rank 1, +60 per rank (300 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Damage + Dexterity contribution + Skill damage − Off-hand deduction Example ·,rank 5·,200 Damage·,100 Dexterity·,31 off-hand weapon Base Damage Use your numbers 734 damage before the target's defenses Dexterity contribution: 100 × 250% = 250, rounded Off-hand deduction: 31 × 50% = 16, rounded up Skill damage: 60 + 60 × (5 − 1) = 300 Damage before defenses: 200 + 250 + 300 − 16 = 734 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Poison Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
