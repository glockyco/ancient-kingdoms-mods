Mechanics Level contribution Your level ÷ 5, rounded downVeteran contribution Total veteran points ÷ 10, rounded downSkill rank Level contribution + veteran contribution, at most the skill's maximum rank Total veteran points are your unspent veteran points plus the ranks of your learned veteran skills. A game defect counts a veteran skill rank that costs 2 points as 1 point. Each such rank lowers your Veteran Level by 1 and the next point's experience cost by 20,000 XP, and a token reset refunds only 1 of its 2 points.
