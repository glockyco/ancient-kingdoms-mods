Cleanse Resistance This debuff has three layers, and cleansing it removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each extra-layer roll starts at 90%. Accuracy increases that chance. See cleanse calculations. While layers remain, each damage tick deals 85% of its full damage at two layers or 70% at one layer.
