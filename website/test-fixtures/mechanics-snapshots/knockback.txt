Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Damage at its level. Skill damage 5Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage Example ·,example caster with 200 Damage at its level·,skill rank 1 205 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 5 = 205 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Knockback Knockback applies only if stun and fear did not apply on the same hit and the target is not already stunned. It pushes the target back1.5m and stuns them for 0.25 seconds. If an obstacle blocks the path, the target also takes 1.25% of the damage just dealt, rounded up to at least 1. The target must be within 15 levels of the attacker, unless the attacker is a boss.
