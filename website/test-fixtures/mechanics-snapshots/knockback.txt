Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Attack Power at its level. Skill damage 5 Damage before defenses Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Attack Power 205 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 5 = 205 The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Knockback A hit rolls against the knockback chance only when it neither stuns nor fears, and only if the target is not already stunned. It pushes the target back 1.5 yd and stuns it for 0.25 seconds. If an obstacle blocks the path, the target also takes 1.25% of the damage just dealt, rounded up to at least 1. Targets 15 or more levels above the attacker cannot be knocked back, unless the attacker is a boss.
