Mechanics Skill Damage Damage is the value in your character window. Ranger Dexterity contribution 150% of Dexterity above 0, rounded Skill damage 1 at rank 1, +4 per rank (17 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Damage + Dexterity contribution + Skill damage − equipped melee weapon damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Damage·,100 Dexterity·,30 equipped melee weapon damage Use your numbers 337 damage before the target's defenses Dexterity contribution: 100 × 150% = 150, rounded Skill damage: 1 + 4 × (5 − 1) = 17 Damage before defenses: 200 + 150 + 17 − 30 = 337 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%.
