Mechanics Debuff Scaling Monster or NPC This debuff receives no attribute bonus. The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has three layers, and cleansing it removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each extra-layer roll starts at 60%. Accuracy increases that chance.
