Mechanics Skill Damage Bard Damage is the value in your character window. Skill damage 1 Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage Example ·,200 Damage Use your numbers 201 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 1 = 201 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
