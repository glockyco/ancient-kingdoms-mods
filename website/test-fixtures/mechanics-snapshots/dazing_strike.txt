The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns.
