Mechanics Skill Damage Wizard Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Skill damage 40 at rank 1, +40 per rank (200 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 400 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 40 + 40 × (5 − 1) = 200 Damage before defenses: 200 + 200 = 400 The target's Cold Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
