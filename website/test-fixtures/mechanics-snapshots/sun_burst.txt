Mechanics Skill Damage Cleric Spell Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 100 at rank 1, +75 per rank (475 at rank 6) Damage before defenses Spell Power + Skill damage Example ·,rank 6·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers 675 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 100 + 75 × (6 − 1) = 475 Damage before defenses: 200 + 475 = 675 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
