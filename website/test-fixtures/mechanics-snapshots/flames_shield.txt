Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Fire Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Damage Shield When a non-spell, single-target attack with a base cast range below 1.5 yd hits the wearer, the attacker takes the Damage Shield amount, not a share of the incoming hit. Scroll skills and weapon effects triggered on hit cannot trigger it. The attacker's Fire Resist reduces this shield's damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. Several shields add together after their individual reductions. The total then varies at random by up to 10% in either direction, with the 10% rounded up, so a 95-damage total ranges from 85 to 105. See damage shields.
