Mechanics The target resists this debuff using Fire Resist. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Damage Shield Damage Shield reflects damage when a non-spell, single-target melee attack with a cast range below 1.5 hits the wearer. Scroll skills and weapon effects triggered on hit cannot trigger it. The shield uses the caster's attribute contribution stored when the buff is applied. See Buff Scaling above for the amount. The reflected hit meets the attacker's matching Defense or resist through resistance and mitigation.
