Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Attack Power at its level. Skill damage 1,500 Damage before defenses Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Attack Power 1,700 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 1,500 = 1,700 The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns. Lifetap Heals the attacker for 100% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 200. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap healing bonuses, such as Lich Shroud's, multiply the healing. See lifetap. Break Armor Each hit that is not blocked, missed or resisted has a 100% chance to trigger Break Armor. When it triggers, the game picks one random equipment slot on the target. If that slot contains an item with durability remaining, the item loses 1–4 durability. Break Armor affects players and mercenaries.
