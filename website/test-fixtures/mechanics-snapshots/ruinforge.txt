See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Stun applies on one roll, but cannot affect a feared target. Another stun extends the existing stun instead of replacing its end time. Bear mounts resist 90% of stun attempts. Lifetap Heals you for 100% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 200. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap Healing bonuses multiply the healing. See lifetap. Break Armor Each hit can trigger Break Armor at the skill's listed chance. When it triggers, the game picks one random equipment slot on the target. If that slot contains an item with durability remaining, the item loses 1–4 durability. Break Armor affects players and mercenaries.
