Mechanics Critical Resist Critical Resist reduces the extra damage from critical hits. The critical multiplier is 1 + (base − 1) × (1 − Critical Resist), where base is 1.5 (or 3 with Radiant Aether). At 100% Critical Resist, a critical hit deals the same damage as an ordinary hit. Each positive point of Dexterity adds 0.05 percentage points of Critical Resist. Gear and buffs can add more, up to a total of 100%.
