Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Magic Damage at its level. Skill damage 2,000 Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Magic Damage 2,200 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 2,000 = 2,200 The target's Cold Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Knockback A hit rolls against the knockback chance only when it neither stuns nor fears, and only if the target is not already stunned. It pushes the target back 1.5 yd and stuns it for 0.25 seconds. If an obstacle blocks the path, the target also takes 1.25% of the damage just dealt, rounded up to at least 1. Targets 15 or more levels above the attacker cannot be knocked back, unless the attacker is a boss.
