Moving target: resist chance −25 percentage points, damage +10% Backstab: resist chance × 0.8 Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Knockback Knockback applies only if stun and fear did not apply on the same hit and the target is not already stunned. It pushes the target back1.5m and stuns them for 0.25 seconds. If an obstacle blocks the path, the target also takes 1.25% of the damage just dealt, rounded up to at least 1. The target must be within 15 levels of the attacker, unless the attacker is a boss.
