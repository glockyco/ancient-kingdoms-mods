Mechanics This skill's rank equals the rank of the skill that summoned the familiar, up to rank 8. Summoned by Invoke Red Familiar. Accuracy Raises the affected character's Accuracy by 8 percentage points at rank 8. The chance that its attacks and debuffs are blocked or resisted falls by the same number of percentage points, within each roll's limits. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
