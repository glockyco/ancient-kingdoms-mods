Mechanics Song Aura Song duration = base duration × (1 + learned song duration bonuses) Active Song Limit Active song limit = 2 + the bonus from Polyphony. A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Song Scaling Song multiplier = 1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2) Fear Resist = min(base value at skill level × song multiplier, 70%) Wisdom does not change this song. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Fear Resist When fear would apply, the target has a chance to resist it. This skill adds at most 70% to that chance after Charisma scaling. Total Fear Resist from all sources is capped at 100%. At 100%, the target resists every fear effect.
