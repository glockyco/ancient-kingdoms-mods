Mechanics Song Aura The song you select keeps recasting itself. Each cast takes 2.5s, shortened by Spell Casting Haste. Each completed cast renews the song for 15s. After you select another song, this one lasts the rest of its duration. Lingering Melody lengthens it by 10% per rank, so at rank 2 it lasts 18s. Active Song Limit A Bard sustains two songs by default (the Active Songs value in your character window). Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the song you renewed longest ago. See Bard Songs. Fear Resist The affected character receives 35 percentage points of Fear Resist at rank 10, before Charisma scaling. When a fear roll succeeds, the target can still resist the fear, with a chance equal to its Fear Resist. This skill adds at most 70 percentage points to that chance after Charisma scaling. Total Fear Resist from all sources is capped at 100%. At 100%, the target resists every fear effect.
