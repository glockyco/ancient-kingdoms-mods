Mechanics Song Aura The song lasts 15s. Learned song-duration bonuses lengthen it by their total percentage, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 18s. Active Song Limit A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Fear Resist When fear would apply, the target has a chance to resist it. This skill adds at most 70 percentage points to that chance after Charisma scaling. Total Fear Resist from all sources is capped at 100%. At 100%, the target resists every fear effect.
