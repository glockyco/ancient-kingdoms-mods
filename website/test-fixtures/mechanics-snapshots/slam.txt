Mechanics Skill Damage Warrior Attack Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 20 at rank 1, +20 per rank (100 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Attack Power Use your numbers 300 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 20 + 20 × (5 − 1) = 100 Damage before defenses: 200 + 100 = 300 The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
