Mechanics Skill Damage Wizard Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Skill damage 14 at rank 1, +14 per rank (70 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 270 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 14 + 14 × (5 − 1) = 70 Damage before defenses: 200 + 70 = 270 The target's Fire Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
