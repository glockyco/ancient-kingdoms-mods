Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 veteran points, capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Damage at its level. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%.
