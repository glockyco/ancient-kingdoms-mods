Mechanics Song Aura The song you select keeps recasting itself. Each cast takes 3.5s, shortened by Spell Casting Haste. Each completed cast renews the song for 20s. After you select another song, this one lasts the rest of its duration. A learned song-duration bonus such as Lingering Melody lengthens it, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 24s. Active Song Limit A Bard sustains two songs by default (the Active Songs value in your character window). Starting another song at the limit ends the song you renewed longest ago. Learning Polyphony removes the limit. See Bard Songs. Song Category: Bard Symphony Song Songs in one category compare strength: the sum of their movement speed, Attack Power, Spell Power, Haste, Spell Casting Haste and Accuracy bonuses. Each bonus except movement speed counts after the singer's Charisma scaling. A target keeps one song of each category, including songs from other Bards and Bard mercenaries. A stronger song replaces a song in the same category. At equal strength, the song already active stays. See Bard Songs.
