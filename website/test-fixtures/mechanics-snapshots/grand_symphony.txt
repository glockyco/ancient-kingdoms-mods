Mechanics Song Aura Song duration = base duration × (1 + learned song duration bonuses) Active Song Limit Active song limit = 2 + the bonus from Polyphony. A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Song Category: Bard Symphony Song Song strength = speed + physical damage % + magic damage % + haste + spell haste + accuracy A target keeps one song of each category, including songs from other Bards and Bard mercenaries. A stronger song replaces a song in the same category. At equal strength, the song already active stays. Song Scaling Song multiplier = 1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2) final value = base value at skill level × song multiplier Wisdom does not change this song. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
