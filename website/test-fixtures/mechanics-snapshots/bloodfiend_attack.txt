Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Attack Power at its level. Skill damage 5 Damage before defenses Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Attack Power 205 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 5 = 205 The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
