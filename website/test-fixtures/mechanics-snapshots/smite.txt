Mechanics Skill Damage Cleric Spell Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 12 at rank 1, +12 per rank (60 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Spell Power + Skill damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers 260 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 12 + 12 × (5 − 1) = 60 Damage before defenses: 200 + 60 = 260 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
