Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Defense gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Monsters marked Immune to Debuffs ignore this skill. Accuracy Lowers the affected character's Accuracy by 10 percentage points at rank 5. The chance that its attacks and debuffs are blocked or resisted rises by the same number of percentage points, within each roll's limits. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
