Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Disease Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Once it lands, the target's Poison Resist reduces each tick by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. Because of a game defect, Poison Resist reduces this disease damage instead of Disease Resist. Cleanse layers and other tick modifiers also change the damage that lands. See damage-over-time calculations. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast.
