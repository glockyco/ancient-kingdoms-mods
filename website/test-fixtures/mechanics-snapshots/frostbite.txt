Mechanics Skill Damage Warrior and Cleric Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Skill damage 1,500 Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 1,700 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 1,500 = 1,700 The target's Cold Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
