Mechanics Skill Damage Necromancer Spell Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 10 at rank 1, +18 per rank (82 at rank 5) Combined damage Spell Power + Skill damage Damage % 85% Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,rank 5·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers 240 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 10 + 18 × (5 − 1) = 82 Combined damage: 200 + 82 = 282 Damage before defenses: 282 × 85% = 240, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
