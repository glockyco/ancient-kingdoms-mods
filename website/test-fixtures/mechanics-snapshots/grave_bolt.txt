Mechanics Skill Damage Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Necromancer Skill damage 10 at rank 1, +18 per rank (82 at rank 5) Combined damage Magic Damage + Skill damage Rank multiplier 85% Damage before defenses Combined damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 5·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 240 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 10 + 18 × (5 − 1) = 82 Combined damage: 200 + 82 = 282 Damage before defenses: 282 × 85% = 240, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely.
