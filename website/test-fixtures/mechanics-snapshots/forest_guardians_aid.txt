Mechanics Skill Damage Damage is the value in your character window. Ranger Dexterity contribution 150% of Dexterity above 0, rounded Skill damage 1,000 at rank 1, +300 per rank (3,700 at rank 10) Damage before defenses Damage + Dexterity contribution + Skill damage Example ·,rank 10·,200 Damage·,100 Dexterity Use your numbers 4,050 damage before the target's defenses Dexterity contribution: 100 × 150% = 150, rounded Skill damage: 1,000 + 300 × (10 − 1) = 3,700 Damage before defenses: 200 + 150 + 3,700 = 4,050 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely.
