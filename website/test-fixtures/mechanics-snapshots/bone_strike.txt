Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 veteran points, capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Damage at its level. Skill damage 8 at rank 1, +4 per rank (84 at rank 20) Combined damage Damage + Skill damage Rank multiplier 100% Damage before defenses Combined damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,example caster with 200 Damage at its level·,skill rank 20 284 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 8 + 4 × (20 − 1) = 84 Combined damage: 200 + 84 = 284 Damage before defenses: 284 × 100% = 284, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%.
