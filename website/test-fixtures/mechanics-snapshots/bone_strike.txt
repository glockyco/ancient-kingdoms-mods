Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 total veteran points (your VT), capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Damage at its level. Skill damage 8 at rank 1, +4 per rank (84 at rank 20) Combined damage Damage + Skill damage Damage % 100% Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,the caster's 200 Damage·,rank 1 208 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 8 + 4 × (1 − 1) = 8 Combined damage: 200 + 8 = 208 Damage before defenses: 208 × 100% = 208, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
