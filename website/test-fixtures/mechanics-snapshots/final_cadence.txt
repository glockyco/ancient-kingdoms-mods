Mechanics Final Cadence You can cast it only while at least three of your songs are active. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Critical heals restore double Health, or triple Health in 10% of cases. Melody counts your active songs when the cast finishes. One critical roll, with a chance equal to the Bard's Critical Strike, applies to the healing of every ally. Its damage hits living, visible, attackable monsters within range in the same zone that are already in combat and are not asleep. Final Cadence does not heal familiars. For each ally healed, healing adds threat equal to a sixth of the Health restored, rounded up, on monsters within 16 yd of the caster that are fighting that ally. The target's Magic Resist reduces the damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist it entirely.
