Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has 10% Cleanse Resist and three layers. Cleansing removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each of those rolls succeeds at 90% × (100% + the cleansing caster's Accuracy), limited to 0–100%. At 100%, one cast removes all three layers. See cleanse calculations. Blindness Blindness covers the affected player's screen with a black overlay for the effect's duration. It changes no combat stats and affects only players. The overlay fades out over 1 second when the effect ends.
