Mechanics Song Aura The song you select keeps recasting itself. Each cast takes 2.5s, shortened by spell haste. Each completed cast renews the song for 12s. After you select another song, this one lasts the rest of its duration. Lingering Melody lengthens it by 10% per rank, so at rank 2 it lasts 14.4s. Active Song Limit A Bard sustains two songs by default (the Active Songs value in your character window). Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the song you renewed longest ago. See Bard Songs. Each level the target is above you adds 2.5 percentage points to its chance to resist. Each point of your Charisma above 0 removes 0.02 percentage points. The final chance is between 0% and 95%. Only living monsters that are not bosses, elites, training dummies, returning home, or immune to debuffs can be charmed. A Bard can control one charmed monster at a time. While you control a charmed monster, casting the song again targets that monster instead of your selected target and rolls its resist again. A resisted recast leaves the existing charm running.
