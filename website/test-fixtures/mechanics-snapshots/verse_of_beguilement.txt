Mechanics Song Aura Song duration = base duration × (1 + learned song duration bonuses) Active Song Limit Active song limit = 2 + the bonus from Polyphony. A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Charm Song multiplier = 1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2) Charmed damage = min(100%, skill value at level × song multiplier) Level difference = target level − Bard level Base resist chance = clamp(target Magic Resist × 0.0005 + clamp(level difference × 0.005, −0.1, 0.1), 0, 0.9) Resist chance = clamp(base resist chance + max(level difference, 0) × 0.025 − max(CHA, 0) × 0.0002, 0, 0.95) Only living monsters that are not bosses, elites, training dummies, or returning home can be charmed. A Bard can control one charmed monster at a time. Casting the song again refreshes that monster before considering the Bard's selected target. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
