Mechanics Song Aura The song lasts 12s. Learned song-duration bonuses lengthen it by their total percentage, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 14.4s. Active Song Limit A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song.
