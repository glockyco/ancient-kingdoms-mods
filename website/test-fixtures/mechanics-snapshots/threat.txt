Mechanics Aggro Skill aggro 4,000 at rank 1, +1,500 per rank (11,500 at rank 6) Health contribution Your maximum Health while Skill aggro is above 0 Aggro added Skill aggro + Health contribution, at most the target's current Health An area skill that delivers its hits as projectiles adds no Health contribution. Example ·,rank 6·,2,000 maximum Health Use your numbers 13,500 aggro before the target's current-Health limit Skill aggro: 4,000 + 1,500 × (6 − 1) = 11,500 Aggro added: 11,500 + 2,000 = 13,500 The skill's tooltip shows only a third of your maximum Health for this bonus, but the game adds all of it.
