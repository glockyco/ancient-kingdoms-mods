Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Magic Damage at its level. Skill damage 1,500 Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Magic Damage 1,700 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 1,500 = 1,700 The target's Fire Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
