Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 total veteran points (your VT), capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Attack Power at its level. Skill damage 3 at rank 1, +3 per rank (60 at rank 20) Damage before defenses Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Attack Power·,rank 1 203 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 3 + 3 × (1 − 1) = 3 Damage before defenses: 200 + 3 = 203 The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
