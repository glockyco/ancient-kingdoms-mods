Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 veteran points, capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Damage at its level. Skill damage 3 at rank 1, +3 per rank (60 at rank 20)Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage Example ·,example caster with 200 Damage at its level·,skill rank 20 260 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 3 + 3 × (20 − 1) = 60Damage before defenses: 200 + 60 = 260 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%.
