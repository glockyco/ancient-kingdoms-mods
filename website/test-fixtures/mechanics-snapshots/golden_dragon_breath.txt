See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Stun applies on one roll, but cannot affect a feared target. Another stun extends the existing stun instead of replacing its end time. Bear mounts resist 90% of stun attempts. Break Armor Each hit can trigger Break Armor at the skill's listed chance. When it triggers, the game picks one random equipment slot on the target. If that slot contains an item with durability remaining, the item loses 1–4 durability. Break Armor affects players and mercenaries.
