Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Magic Damage at its level. Skill damage 12,000 Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Magic Damage 12,200 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 12,000 = 12,200 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns. Break Armor Each hit that is not blocked, missed or resisted has a 50% chance to trigger Break Armor. When it triggers, the game picks one random equipment slot on the target. If that slot contains an item with durability remaining, the item loses 1–4 durability. Break Armor affects players and mercenaries.
