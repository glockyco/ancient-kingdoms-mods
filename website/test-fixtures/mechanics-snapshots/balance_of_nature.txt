Mechanics Balance Health Sets you and every living, visible player, companion and mercenary within 10 yd, in your party or not, to their average Health percentage, applied to each character's own maximum Health and rounded. Characters above the average lose Health. For example, 100% and 25% average to 62.5%, so a character with 2,000 maximum Health ends at 1,250 Health. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2.
