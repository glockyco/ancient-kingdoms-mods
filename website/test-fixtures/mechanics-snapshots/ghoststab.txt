Mechanics Skill Damage Rogue Attack Power is the value in your character window. Enter Base Damage from the weapon's tooltip, even if the weapon is broken. Enter 0 if no weapon is equipped in the named slot. Dexterity contribution 250% of Dexterity above 0, rounded Off-hand deduction Off-hand weapon's Base Damage × 50%, rounded up Skill damage 300 at rank 1, +150 per rank (1,050 at rank 6) Damage before defenses Attack Power + Dexterity contribution + Skill damage − Off-hand deduction Example ·,rank 6·,200 Attack Power·,100 Dexterity·,31 off-hand weapon Base Damage Use your numbers 1,484 damage before the target's defenses Dexterity contribution: 100 × 250% = 250, rounded Off-hand deduction: 31 × 50% = 16, rounded up Skill damage: 300 + 150 × (6 − 1) = 1,050 Damage before defenses: 200 + 250 + 1,050 − 16 = 1,484 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Poison Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Threat Each hit on a monster lowers the attacker's threat by 5,000 at rank 1, +3,000 per rank (20,000 at rank 6), but not below 1. The hit's damage adds no threat. Against an NPC, the hit's damage and crowd-control threat offset the reduction, and the attacker's threat can fall below 1. See threat.
