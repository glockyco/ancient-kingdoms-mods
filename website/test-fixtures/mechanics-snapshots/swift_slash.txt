Mechanics Skill Damage Ranger Attack Power is the value in your character window. Enter Base Damage from the weapon's tooltip, even if the weapon is broken. Enter 0 if no weapon is equipped in the named slot. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. With Wild Strike active, the Ranger's next hit deals 10% more per Wild Strike rank, rounded, as Magic damage. Apply this bonus after the calculation above. Magic Resist reduces that hit by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, and adds 5 percentage points per 100 points to its chance to be resisted.
