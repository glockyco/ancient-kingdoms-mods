Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Attack Power at its level. Skill damage 1,200 Damage before defenses Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Attack Power 1,400 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 1,200 = 1,400 The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals the attacker for 100% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 200. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap healing bonuses, such as Lich Shroud's, multiply the healing. See lifetap.
