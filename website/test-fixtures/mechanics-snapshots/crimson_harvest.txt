Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Damage at its level. Skill damage 1,200 Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage Example ·,example caster with 200 Damage at its level·,skill rank 1 1,400 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 1,200 = 1,400 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals you for 100% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 200. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap Healing bonuses multiply the healing. See lifetap.
