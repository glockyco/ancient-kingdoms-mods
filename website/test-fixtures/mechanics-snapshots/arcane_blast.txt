Mechanics Skill Damage Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Wizard Rank multiplier 200% at rank 1, +50% per rank (400% at rank 5) Damage before defenses Magic Damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 5·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 800 damage before the target's defenses Rank multiplier: 200% + 50% × (5 − 1) = 400% Damage before defenses: 200 × 400% = 800, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely.
