Mechanics Damage-over-Time Execute While the target is below 30% of its maximum Health, your damage-over-time ticks deal 5% more, rounded, so a 200-damage tick deals 210 at rank 1. Each rank adds 5%, reaching 25% at rank 5. Learned execute bonuses add together. See damage over time for the full tick. You must remain in the game as the player who applied the debuff to another character. Ticks from pets and monsters receive no bonus.
