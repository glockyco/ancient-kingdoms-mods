Mechanics Damage-over-Time Execute Adds 5% × skill lvl to your debuff ticks when the target is below 30% maximum Health. Learned execute bonuses add together. Tick = round(resisted, counter-scaled damage × (1 + summed execute bonuses)), after any Reaper Form bonus and before critical scaling. You must remain in the game as the player who applied the debuff to another character. Ticks from pets and monsters receive no bonus.
