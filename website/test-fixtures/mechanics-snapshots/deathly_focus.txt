Mechanics Damage-over-Time Execute While the target is below 30% of its maximum Health, your damage-over-time ticks deal 25% more damage, rounded, so a 200-damage tick deals 250 at rank 5. Rank 1 gives 5%, and each rank adds 5%. Learned execute bonuses add together. See damage over time for the full tick. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Only damage-over-time effects you apply to another character get the bonus, and only while you are still in the game. Ticks from companions, mercenaries and monsters get none.
