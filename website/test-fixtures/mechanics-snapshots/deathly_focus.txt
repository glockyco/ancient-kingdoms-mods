Mechanics Damage-over-Time Execute Adds 5% × skill lvl to your debuff ticks when the target is strictly below 30% maximum Health. Learned execute bonuses add together. Tick = round(resisted, counter-scaled damage × (1 + summed execute bonuses)), then critical scaling. Requires you to remain spawned as the external player source of the debuff. Pet- and monster-sourced ticks do not receive the bonus.
