Mechanics Damage-over-Time Execute While the target is below 30% of its maximum Health, your damage-over-time ticks deal 25% more damage, rounded, so a 200-damage tick deals 250 at rank 5. Rank 1 gives 5%, and each rank adds 5%. Learned execute bonuses add together. See damage over time for the full tick. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. You must remain in the game as the player who applied the debuff to another character. Ticks from pets and monsters receive no bonus.
