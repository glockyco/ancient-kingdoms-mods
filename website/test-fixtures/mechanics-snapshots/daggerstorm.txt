Mechanics Skill Damage Damage is the value in your character window. Rogue Rank multiplier 150% at rank 1, +30% per rank (270% at rank 5)Damage before defenses Damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 5·,200 Damage Use your numbers 540 damage before the target's defenses Rank multiplier: 150% + 30% × (5 − 1) = 270%Damage before defenses: 200 × 270% = 540, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%.
