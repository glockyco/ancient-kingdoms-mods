Mechanics Death Bloom Health cost C = ceil(max Health × 50%) Base heal = round(C × (137.5% + 12.5% × skill lvl + max(INT, 0) × 0.1%)) Targets another living player or a non-familiar pet, including mercenaries and other players' combat pets, within 8 yd. Cannot target yourself, familiars, monsters or NPCs. Your current Health must exceed C. Pays the full cost even if the target is already at maximum Health. Wisdom does not increase the heal. Your critical chance applies to this heal. Critical heals restore double Health, or triple Health in 10% of cases. Healing is capped by the target's missing Health. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
