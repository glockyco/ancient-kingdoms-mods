Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Wisdom does not increase this heal. Targets another living player or a non-familiar pet, including mercenaries and other players' companions, within 8 yd. It cannot target you, familiars, monsters or NPCs. Your current Health must be above the Health spent. You pay the full cost even if the target is already at maximum Health. Your Critical Strike is the chance that this heal is critical. Critical heals restore double Health, or triple Health in 10% of cases. Healing is capped by the target's missing Health.
