Targets another living player or a non-familiar pet, including mercenaries and other players' companions, within 8 yd. Cannot target yourself, familiars, monsters or NPCs. Your current Health must be above the Health spent. You pay the full cost even if the target is already at maximum Health. Your critical chance applies to this heal. Critical heals restore double Health, or triple Health in 10% of cases. Healing is capped by the target's missing Health. Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 1s cast into 0.8s.
