Mechanics Death Bloom Health cost C = ceil(max Health × 50%) Base heal = round(C × (137.5% + 12.5% × skill lvl + max(INT, 0) × 0.001)) Targets another living player or a non-familiar pet, including mercenaries and other players' combat pets, within 8m. Cannot target yourself, familiars, monsters or NPCs. Your current Health must exceed C. Pays the full cost even if the target is already at maximum Health. Wisdom does not increase the heal. At your critical chance, the heal is doubled; 10% of critical heals are tripled instead. Healing is capped by the target's missing Health. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
