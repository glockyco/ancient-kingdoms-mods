Mechanics Debuff Scaling Monster or NPC This debuff receives no attribute bonus. The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has three layers, and cleansing it removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each extra-layer roll starts at 50%. Accuracy increases that chance. See cleanse calculations. While layers remain, each damage tick deals 85% of its full damage at two layers or 70% at one layer. Blindness Blindness covers the affected player's screen with a black overlay for the effect's duration. It changes no combat stats and affects only players. The overlay fades out over 1 second when the effect ends.
