No pulse occurs at the start. Mana and energy are paid at the start and are not refunded on interruption. Moving more than 0.01 yd from your starting position, losing the target, range or sight, stun or fear cancels the channel. Cancellation starts the full cooldown immediately, including when a pulse kills the target. Using another skill cancels the channel, starts its full cooldown immediately and starts the new skill. You must be free of stun and fear. The new skill must be active, meet its casting requirements and have a valid target within range and sight when required. Skill Damage Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals you for 60% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 120. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap Healing bonuses multiply the healing. See lifetap.
