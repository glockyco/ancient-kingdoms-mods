No pulse occurs at the start. Mana and energy are paid at the start and are not refunded on interruption. Moving more than 0.01 units from your starting position, losing the target, range or sight, stun or fear cancels the channel. Cancellation starts the full cooldown immediately, including when a pulse kills the target. Using another skill cancels the channel, starts its full cooldown immediately and starts the new skill. You must be free of stun and fear. The new skill must be active, meet its casting requirements and have a valid target within range and sight when required.
