Mechanics Skill Damage Necromancer Spell Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 20 at rank 1, +20 per rank (160 at rank 8) Combined damage Spell Power + Skill damage Damage % 80% at rank 1, +10% per rank (150% at rank 8) Total damage Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Damage per pulse Total damage ÷ 3, rounded down, plus 1 on each of the first pulses until the 3 pulses add up to Total damage Example ·,rank 8·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers Pulses deal 180, 180 and 180 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 20 + 20 × (8 − 1) = 160 Combined damage: 200 + 160 = 360 Damage %: 80% + 10% × (8 − 1) = 150% Total damage: 360 × 150% = 540, rounded Damage per pulse: 540 ÷ 3 = 180 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Channel: 3 Pulses Base cast time 3s Channel time Base cast time × (100% − Spell Casting Haste), at least 0.05s Pulse interval Channel time ÷ 3. The first pulse lands one interval after the channel starts, and the last when it ends Example ·,3s cast time·,20% Spell Casting Haste Use your numbers Pulses land at 0.8s, 1.6s and 2.4s Channel time: 3s × (100% − 20%) = 2.4s Pulse interval: 2.4s ÷ 3 = 0.8s Each pulse recalculates the skill's damage from your current stats and splits it as Skill Damage above shows. Each pulse is a separate hit, so the target's defenses, resist roll and critical hits apply to each pulse. Mana and energy are paid at the start and are not refunded on interruption. Moving more than 0.01 yd from your starting position, losing the target, range or sight, stun or fear cancels the channel. Cancellation starts the full cooldown immediately, including when a pulse kills the target. Using another skill cancels the channel, starts its full cooldown immediately and starts the new skill. You must be free of stun and fear. The new skill must be active, meet its casting requirements and have a valid target within range and sight when required. Lifetap Heals the attacker for 60% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 120. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap healing bonuses, such as Lich Shroud's, multiply the healing. See lifetap.
