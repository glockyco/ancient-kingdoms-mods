Mechanics Channel: 3 Pulses Channel time cast time × (1 − spell haste), at least 0.05sPulse times 3 pulses, one at the end of each third of the channel timePulse damage full skill damage split into 3 whole-number parts, with any remainder added 1 at a time to the earliest pulses Example ·,3s cast time·,20% spell haste·,1,000 full skill damage Pulses deal 334 damage at 0.8s, 333 at 1.6s, 333 at 2.4s Channel time: 3s × (1 − 20%) = 2.4sPulse damage: 1,000 ÷ 3 = 333 with 1 left over Full skill damage is recalculated before mitigation for each pulse. No pulse occurs at the start. Mana and energy are paid at the start and are not refunded on interruption. Moving more than 0.01 units from your starting position, losing the target, range or sight, stun or fear cancels the channel. Cancellation starts the full cooldown immediately, including when a pulse kills the target. Using another skill cancels the channel, starts its full cooldown immediately and starts the new skill. You must be free of stun and fear. The new skill must be active, meet its casting requirements and have a valid target within range and sight when required.
