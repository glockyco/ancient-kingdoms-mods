Skill damage 150 Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,example caster with 200 Magic Damage at its level·,skill rank 1 350 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 150 = 350 The target's Poison Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns.
