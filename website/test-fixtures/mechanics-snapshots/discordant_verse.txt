Mechanics Song Aura The song you select keeps recasting itself. Each cast takes 2.5s, shortened by spell haste. Each completed cast renews the song for 20s. After you select another song, this one lasts the rest of its duration. Lingering Melody lengthens it by 10% per rank, so at rank 2 it lasts 24s. Active Song Limit A Bard sustains two songs by default (the Active Songs value in your character window). Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the song you renewed longest ago. See Bard Songs.
