Mechanics Scroll Mastery A scroll casts this skill at rank Scroll Mastery ÷ 5, rounded, from rank 1 to rank 20, so 37% Scroll Mastery casts rank 7. The Rank Scaling table lists the mastery each rank needs. The target resists this debuff using Magic Resist. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Dispel Mechanics You cast this on a monster to remove its beneficial buffs. First the monster can resist the skill, then each buff has a separate removal chance. 1. Resist. Your Accuracy lowers the monster's chance to resist this skill (shown above). If the monster resists, no buffs are removed. 2. Remove buffs. Each buff has a separate removal roll. The chance to remove a buff is 100% minus its Dispel Resist, plus your Dispel Resist reduction. A buff with 0% Dispel Resist is always removed. Each effective scroll rank lowers Dispel Resist by 1 percentage point. Your reduction grows with your Scroll Mastery rank, lowering a monster's Dispel Resist by 1 percentage point per effective rank (up to 20). Accuracy helps the scroll land in step 1. Scroll Mastery removes more buffs in step 2.
