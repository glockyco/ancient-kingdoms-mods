Mechanics Scroll Mastery Skill level = clamp(round(Scroll Mastery% ÷ 5), 1, 20) Resist Chance clamp( target's Magic Resist × 0.0005 + clamp((target level − skill user level) × 0.005, −0.1, 0.1) − skill user's Accuracy , 0, 0.9) Every 100 points of the target's matching Resist add 5 percentage points to resist chance before level difference and Accuracy. Dispel Mechanics You cast this on a monster to remove its beneficial buffs. First the monster can resist the skill, then each buff has a separate removal chance. 1. Resist. Your Accuracy lowers the monster's chance to resist this skill (shown above). If the monster resists, no buffs are removed. 2. Remove buffs. Each buff has a separate removal roll. The chance to remove a buff is 100% minus its Dispel Resist, plus your Dispel Resist reduction. A buff with 0% Dispel Resist is always removed. Dispel Resist reduction = clamp(round(Mastery% ÷ 5), 1, 20) × 0.01 Your reduction grows with your Scroll Mastery rank, lowering a monster's Dispel Resist by 1 percentage point per effective rank (up to 20). Accuracy helps the scroll land in step 1. Scroll Mastery removes more buffs in step 2.
