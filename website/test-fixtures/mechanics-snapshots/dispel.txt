Mechanics Scroll Mastery A scroll casts this skill at one rank per 5 percentage points of Scroll Mastery, rounded, from rank 1 up to rank 20. At 37% Scroll Mastery, it casts at rank 7. The Rank Scaling table lists the mastery each rank needs. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this skill, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Dispel Mechanics You cast this on a monster to remove its beneficial buffs. First the monster can resist the skill, then each buff has a separate removal chance. 1. Resist. The monster rolls to resist this skill with its Magic Resist, and your Accuracy lowers the chance. If the monster resists, no buffs are removed. 2. Remove buffs. Each buff has a separate removal roll. The chance to remove a buff is 100% minus its Dispel Resist, plus your Dispel Resist reduction. A buff with 0% Dispel Resist is always removed. Each effective scroll rank lowers Dispel Resist by 1 percentage point. Your reduction grows with your Scroll Mastery rank, lowering a monster's Dispel Resist by 1 percentage point per effective rank (up to 20). Accuracy helps the scroll land in step 1. Scroll Mastery removes more buffs in step 2.
