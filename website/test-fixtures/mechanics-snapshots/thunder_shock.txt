Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Spell Power at its level. Skill damage 500 Damage before defenses Spell Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Spell Power 700 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 500 = 700 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns.
