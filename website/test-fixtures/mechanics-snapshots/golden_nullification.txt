Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this skill, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Dispel Mechanics Monsters use this to remove beneficial buffs from you and your pets. First you can resist the skill, then it removes your buffs. 1. Resist. You roll to resist this skill with your Magic Resist, and the monster's Accuracy lowers the chance. If you resist, your buffs stay. 2. Remove buffs. If the skill lands, it removes your skill, food, potion and relic buffs except Rest. Bard songs stay. On a pet it removes the same buffs, including Rest. Bard songs stay.
