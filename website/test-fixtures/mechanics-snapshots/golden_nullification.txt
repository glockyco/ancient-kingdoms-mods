Mechanics The target resists this debuff using Magic Resist. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Dispel Mechanics Monsters use this to remove beneficial buffs from you and your pets. First you can resist the skill, then it removes your buffs. 1. Resist. You roll to resist this skill (chance shown above). The monster's Accuracy lowers your chance to resist. Matching resistance raises it. If you resist, your buffs stay. 2. Remove buffs. If the skill lands, it removes all your buffs except Rest. It removes all buffs from an affected pet.
