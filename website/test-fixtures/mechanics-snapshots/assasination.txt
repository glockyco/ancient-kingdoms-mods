Mechanics Skill Damage Rogue Attack Power is the value in your character window. Enter Base Damage from the weapon's tooltip, even if the weapon is broken. Enter 0 if no weapon is equipped in the named slot. Off-hand deduction Off-hand weapon's Base Damage × 50%, rounded up Combined damage Attack Power − Off-hand deduction Damage % 1000% Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,200 Attack Power·,31 off-hand weapon Base Damage Use your numbers 1,840 damage before the target's defenses Off-hand deduction: 31 × 50% = 16, rounded up Combined damage: 200 − 16 = 184 Damage before defenses: 184 × 1000% = 1,840, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. You can cast this skill only when the target's Health is at most 25% of its maximum Health, rounded to the nearest whole point.
