Mechanics Scroll Mastery A scroll casts this skill at one rank per 5 percentage points of Scroll Mastery, rounded, from rank 1 up to rank 20. At 37% Scroll Mastery, it casts at rank 7. The Rank Scaling table lists the mastery each rank needs. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2.
