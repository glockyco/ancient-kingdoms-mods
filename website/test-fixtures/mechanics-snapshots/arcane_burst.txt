Mechanics When used by a mercenary, skill level = floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10), capped at max skill level. Damage Formula Pre-Mitigation Skill Damage + Magic Damage Magic Damage (round(INT × 1.5) + casting weapon magic damage + other equipment magic damage) × (1 + passive damage bonus + buff damage bonus) Multiply the damage before reductions by 140% + 10% × skill lvl. How Damage Is Calculated Variance: ×0.9–1.1 Backstab: +10% damage when attacking from behind (Rogues with Improved Backstab: +25% instead) Level difference: ±2% per level (max ±20%) Slayer reduction (boss or elite attacking a player or pet): −Slayer level × 10% Enrage: non-spell attacks by monsters below 10% health deal 50–75% more damage. Damage reduction: −ceil(damage × clamp(target's Magic Resist × 0.0005, 0, 0.9)) Critical hit: ×1.5 Radiant Aether (15% on a critical hit, consumes one item): ×2 again, for ×3 total Every 100 points of the matching Resist reduce the damage you take from a hit by 5%, up to a 90% reduction before rounding. Resist Chance clamp( target's Magic Resist × 0.0005 + (target level − attacker level) × 0.005 − attacker's Accuracy , 0, 0.9) Every 100 points of the matching Resist add 5 percentage points to resist chance before Accuracy and level difference. Moving target: resist chance −25 percentage points, damage +10% Backstab: resist chance × 0.8 Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
