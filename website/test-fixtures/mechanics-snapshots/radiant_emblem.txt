Mechanics Cleanse Mechanics Cast on yourself or an ally, this skill removes harmful debuffs of the types it cleanses. A cleanse removes only debuffs of the types it lists: poison, disease, cold, fire or magic. A debuff with none of those types, such as one that Defense resists, cannot be cleansed. The target cannot resist the cleanse. See cleanse calculations for layer removal and damage-over-time scaling.
