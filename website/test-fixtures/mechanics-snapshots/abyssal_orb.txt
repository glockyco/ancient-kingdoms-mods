Mechanics Skill Damage Monster or NPC The caster uses its own Spell Power at its level. Skill damage 150 Damage before defenses Spell Power + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Spell Power 350 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 150 = 350 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
