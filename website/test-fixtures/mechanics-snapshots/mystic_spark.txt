Mechanics Skill Damage Wizard Spell Power is the value in your character window. Damage % 50% Damage before defenses Spell Power × Damage %, rounded Example ·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers 100 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 × 50% = 100, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
