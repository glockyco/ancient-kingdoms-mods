Mechanics Skill Damage Warrior Damage is the value in your character window. Skill damage 1 Combined damage Damage + Skill damage Damage % 300% at rank 1, +40% per rank (500% at rank 6) Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,rank 6·,200 Damage Use your numbers 1,005 damage before the target's defenses Combined damage: 200 + 1 = 201 Damage %: 300% + 40% × (6 − 1) = 500% Damage before defenses: 201 × 500% = 1,005, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
