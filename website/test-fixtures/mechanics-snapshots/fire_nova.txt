Mechanics Scroll Mastery A scroll casts this skill at one rank per 5 percentage points of Scroll Mastery, rounded, from rank 1 up to rank 20. At 37% Scroll Mastery, it casts at rank 7. The Rank Scaling table lists the mastery each rank needs. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Skill Damage Player Scroll casts use the skill's damage without your Attack Power or Spell Power. Damage before defenses 1,000 at rank 1, +250 per rank (5,750 at rank 20) Example ·,rank 20 Use your numbers 5,750 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 1,000 + 250 × (20 − 1) = 5,750 The target's Fire Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
