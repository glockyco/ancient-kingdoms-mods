Mechanics Wild Strike Your next auto attack, Swift Slash with any melee weapon or Archer Shot, consumes the buff. After its ordinary damage calculation, the hit deals 10% more per Wild Strike rank, rounded, and becomes Magic damage. Magic Resist reduces it by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%. At rank 5, a 200-damage auto attack deals 300. See Wild Strike, the damage pipeline and resistance. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Use the Swift Slash or Archer Shot calculator for the auto attack's damage, then multiply it by 100% + 10% per Wild Strike rank, rounded, before the target's defenses.
