Mechanics Wild Strike The next sword or bow auto attack consumes the buff. After its ordinary damage calculation, the hit deals 10% more per Wild Strike rank, rounded, and becomes Magic damage that meets Magic Resist. A 200-damage auto attack deals 220 at rank 1 and 300 at rank 5. See Wild Strike, the damage pipeline and resistance. Use the Swift Slash or Archer Shot calculator while Wild Strike is active to find the auto attack's damage before the target's defenses. Your character-window Damage already includes this buff's flat Damage bonus.
