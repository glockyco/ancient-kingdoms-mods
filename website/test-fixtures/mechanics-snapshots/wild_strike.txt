Mechanics Wild Strike The next sword or bow auto attack consumes the buff. After its ordinary damage calculation, the hit deals 10% more per Wild Strike rank, rounded, and becomes Magic damage, which Magic Resist reduces. At rank 5, a 200-damage auto attack deals 300. See Wild Strike, the damage pipeline and resistance. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Use the Swift Slash or Archer Shot calculator while Wild Strike is active to find the auto attack's damage before the target's defenses. Your character-window Damage already includes this buff's flat Damage bonus.
