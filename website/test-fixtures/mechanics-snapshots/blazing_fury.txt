Mechanics Accuracy Raises the affected character's Accuracy by 25 percentage points at rank 1. The chance that its attacks and debuffs are blocked or resisted falls by the same number of percentage points, within each roll's limits. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%. Critical Strike Raises the affected character's Critical Strike by 25 percentage points at rank 1, to at most 70%. See critical hits for the extra damage.
