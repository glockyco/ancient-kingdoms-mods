Mechanics Skill Damage Bard Magic Damage and Damage are the values in your character window. Combined damage Magic Damage + Damage Damage % 150% at rank 1, +50% per rank (350% at rank 5) Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,rank 5·,200 Magic Damage·,200 Damage Use your numbers 1,400 damage before the target's defenses Combined damage: 200 + 200 = 400 Damage %: 150% + 50% × (5 − 1) = 350% Damage before defenses: 400 × 350% = 1,400, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
