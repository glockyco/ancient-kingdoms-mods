Mechanics Healing Warrior, Cleric, Rogue, Wizard, Druid, Bard, and Necromancer Skill healing 1,400 at rank 1, +400 per rank (5,000 at rank 10) Wisdom percentage 0.4% per point of Wisdom above 0, at most 500% Wisdom healing Skill healing × Wisdom percentage, rounded Health restored Skill healing + Wisdom healing, at most the recipient's missing Health Mana or Rage restored 1,000 at rank 1, +250 per rank (3,250 at rank 10), at most the recipient's missing Mana or Rage Example ·,rank 10·,100 Wisdom Use your numbers 7,000 Health before critical heals and the missing-Health cap, and 3,250 Mana (Rage for Warrior and Rogue mercenaries), capped at what is missing Skill healing: 1,400 + 400 × (10 − 1) = 5,000 Wisdom percentage: 100 × 0.4% = 40% Wisdom healing: 5,000 × 40% = 2,000, rounded Health restored: 5,000 + 2,000 = 7,000 Mana or Rage restored: 1,000 + 250 × (10 − 1) = 3,250 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. A heal is critical at the caster's Critical Chance. A critical heal restores double Health, and 1 in 10 critical heals restores triple, still capped by missing Health. Your Critical Chance is shown in the character window. An area heal rolls once for all recipients.
