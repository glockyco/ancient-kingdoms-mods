Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Once it lands, the target's Magic Resist reduces each tick by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. Cleanse layers and other tick modifiers also change the damage that lands. See damage-over-time calculations. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has 10% Cleanse Resist and three layers. Cleansing removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each of those rolls succeeds at 90% × (100% + the cleansing caster's Accuracy), limited to 0–100%. At 100%, one cast removes all three layers. See cleanse calculations. After a cleanse removes layers, each damage tick deals 85% with two layers left and 70% with one, rounded up. Root Root stops movement and does not break when the target takes damage. A rooted companion or mercenary has a 10% chance to break free every second. A rooted player has no automatic break-free roll.
