Mechanics Song Aura The song lasts 20s. Learned song-duration bonuses lengthen it by their total percentage, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 24s. Active Song Limit A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Song Category: Bard Focus Song Song strength adds its movement speed, physical damage percentage, magic damage percentage, Haste, spell haste and Accuracy bonuses. A target keeps one song of each category, including songs from other Bards and Bard mercenaries. A stronger song replaces a song in the same category. At equal strength, the song already active stays. Accuracy This skill's Accuracy bonus lowers the target's chance to block or resist an attack by the same number of percentage points. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
