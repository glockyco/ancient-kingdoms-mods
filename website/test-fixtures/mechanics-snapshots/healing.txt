Mechanics Healing Cleric Skill healing 250 at rank 1, +250 per rank (1,250 at rank 5) Wisdom percentage 0.4% per point of Wisdom above 0, at most 500% Wisdom healing Skill healing × Wisdom percentage, rounded Health restored Skill healing + Wisdom healing, at most the recipient's missing Health Example ·,rank 5·,100 Wisdom Use your numbers 1,750 Health before critical heals and the missing-Health cap Skill healing: 250 + 250 × (5 − 1) = 1,250 Wisdom percentage: 100 × 0.4% = 40% Wisdom healing: 1,250 × 40% = 500, rounded Health restored: 1,250 + 500 = 1,750 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The caster's Critical Strike is the chance that a heal is critical. A critical heal restores double Health, and 1 in 10 critical heals restores triple, still capped by missing Health. Your Critical Strike is shown in the character window.
