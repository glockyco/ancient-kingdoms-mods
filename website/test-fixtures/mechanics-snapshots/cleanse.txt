Mechanics Cleanse Mechanics Cast on yourself or an ally, this skill removes harmful debuffs of the types it cleanses. The target cannot resist the cleanse. Each matching debuff has three layers, and the cleanse removes the debuff only after all three are gone. If the debuff has 0% Cleanse Resist, one cast removes all three layers. Otherwise, the cast always removes one layer and rolls twice to remove one additional layer per roll. Each roll has a (100% − Cleanse Resist) × (1 + the cleanser's Accuracy) chance, capped at 100%. A debuff with 100% Cleanse Resist cannot be cleansed. For damage-over-time debuffs, each tick deals 85% of its full damage at two layers or 70% at one layer. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
