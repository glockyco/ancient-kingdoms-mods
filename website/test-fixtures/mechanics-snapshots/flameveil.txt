Mechanics Damage Shield When a non-spell, single-target attack with a base cast range below 1.5 yd hits the wearer, the attacker takes the Damage Shield amount, not a share of the incoming hit. Scroll skills and weapon effects triggered on hit cannot trigger it. Defense and resist stats do not reduce this shield's damage. Several shields add together after their individual reductions. The total then varies at random by up to 10% in either direction, with the 10% rounded up, so a 95-damage total ranges from 85 to 105. See resistance and mitigation.
