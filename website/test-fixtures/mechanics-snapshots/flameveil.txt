Mechanics Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Damage Shield Damage Shield reflects damage when a non-spell, single-target melee attack with a cast range below 1.5 hits the wearer. Scroll skills and weapon effects triggered on hit cannot trigger it. Reflected damage increases by 0.75 per point of the wearer's Wisdom. Every 100 points of the attacker's matching Resist reduce reflected damage by 5%, up to a 90% reduction. The final damage varies randomly by ±10%.
