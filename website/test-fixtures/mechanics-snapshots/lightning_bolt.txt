Mechanics Scroll Mastery A scroll casts this skill at one rank per 5 percentage points of Scroll Mastery, rounded, from rank 1 up to rank 20. At 37% Scroll Mastery, it casts at rank 7. The Rank Scaling table lists the mastery each rank needs. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Skill Damage Player Scroll casts use the skill's damage without your Damage or Magic Damage. Damage before defenses 2,000 at rank 1, +300 per rank (7,700 at rank 20) Example ·,rank 20 Use your numbers 7,700 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 2,000 + 300 × (20 − 1) = 7,700 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
