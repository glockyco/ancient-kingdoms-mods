Mechanics Song Aura activeDuration = baseDuration × (1 + total learned song duration bonuses) recipient check interval = 0.5s The game re-evaluates nearby eligible recipients while the song remains active. A recipient gains the effect on entering the aura and loses it on leaving the aura. Active Song Limit maximumActiveSongs = 2 + learned Polyphony bonus A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting a song while at the current limit ends the oldest active song.
