Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 veteran points, capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Magic Damage at its level. Skill damage 90 at rank 1, +15 per rank (375 at rank 20) Combined damage Magic Damage + Skill damage Rank multiplier 150% Damage before defenses Combined damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,example caster with 200 Magic Damage at its level·,skill rank 20 862 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 90 + 15 × (20 − 1) = 375 Combined damage: 200 + 375 = 575 Damage before defenses: 575 × 150% = 862, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Cold Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals you for 30% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 60. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts.
