Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 total veteran points (your VT), capped at the skill's maximum rank. Resist Chance The target's Armor Class gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Once it lands, the target's Armor Class reduces each tick by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%. See damage-over-time calculations. Monsters whose pages list Immune to Debuffs are unaffected, except bosses. A boss's immunity lets through debuffs that only deal damage over time.
