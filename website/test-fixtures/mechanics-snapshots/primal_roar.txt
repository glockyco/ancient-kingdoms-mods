Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 veteran points, capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Damage at its level. Skill damage 100 at rank 1, +20 per rank (480 at rank 20) Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage Example ·,example caster with 200 Damage at its level·,skill rank 20 680 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 100 + 20 × (20 − 1) = 480 Damage before defenses: 200 + 480 = 680 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns.
