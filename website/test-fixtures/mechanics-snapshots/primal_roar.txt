Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 total veteran points (your VT), capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Damage at its level. Skill damage 100 at rank 1, +20 per rank (480 at rank 20) Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Damage·,rank 0 280 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 100 + 20 × (0 − 1) = 80 Damage before defenses: 200 + 80 = 280 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
