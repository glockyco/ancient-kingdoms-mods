Mechanics Skill Damage Ranger Attack Power is the value in your character window. Enter your main-hand weapon's Base Damage from its tooltip. Enter 0 if it is broken or your main hand is empty. Dexterity contribution 150% of Dexterity above 0, rounded Skill damage 10 at rank 1, +10 per rank (50 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Attack Power + Dexterity contribution + Skill damage − main-hand weapon Base Damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Attack Power·,100 Dexterity·,30 main-hand weapon Base Damage Use your numbers 370 damage before the target's defenses Dexterity contribution: 100 × 150% = 150, rounded Skill damage: 10 + 10 × (5 − 1) = 50 Damage before defenses: 200 + 150 + 50 − 30 = 370 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
