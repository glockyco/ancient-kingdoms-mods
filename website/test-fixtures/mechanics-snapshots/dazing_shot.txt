Mechanics Skill Damage Damage is the value in your character window. Ranger Dexterity contribution 150% of Dexterity above 0, rounded Skill damage 10 at rank 1, +10 per rank (50 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Damage + Dexterity contribution + Skill damage − equipped melee weapon damage Example ·,rank 5·,200 Damage·,100 Dexterity·,30 equipped melee weapon damage Use your numbers 370 damage before the target's defenses Dexterity contribution: 100 × 150% = 150, rounded Skill damage: 10 + 10 × (5 − 1) = 50 Damage before defenses: 200 + 150 + 50 − 30 = 370 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns.
