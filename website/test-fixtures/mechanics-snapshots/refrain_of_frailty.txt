Mechanics Song Aura The song you select keeps recasting itself. Each cast takes 3.5s, shortened by Spell Casting Haste. Each completed cast renews the song for 20s. After you select another song, this one lasts the rest of its duration. A learned song-duration bonus such as Lingering Melody lengthens it, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 24s. Active Song Limit A Bard sustains two songs by default (the Active Songs value in your character window). Starting another song at the limit ends the song you renewed longest ago. Learning Polyphony removes the limit. See Bard Songs. Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Monsters whose pages list Immune to Debuffs are still affected, except bosses. Resist Chance Reduction This skill bypasses monsters' immunity to debuffs. After the ordinary resist chance is capped at 90%, it lowers that chance by 30 percentage points, to at least 0%, before the resist roll.
