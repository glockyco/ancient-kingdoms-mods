Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Damage Shield Damage Shield reflects damage when a non-spell, single-target melee attack with a cast range below 1.5 hits the wearer. Scroll skills and weapon effects triggered on hit cannot trigger it. The shield uses the caster's attribute contribution stored when the buff is applied. See Buff Scaling above for the amount. The attacker's Defense or matching resist reduces the reflected hit. See resistance and mitigation.
