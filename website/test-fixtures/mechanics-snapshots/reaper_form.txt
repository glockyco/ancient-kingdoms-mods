Mechanics Reaper Form Toggle with no time limit. Health drain per second = round(max Health × 5%) Drain ignores resistance, wards and critical hits, and does not break mesmerize. Ends when toggled off or mounted. Death removes the form unless its Remain After Death flag is set (not set for this skill). The form persists across relog. The barber cannot change your appearance while the form is active. Using the skill again dismisses it immediately, even while feared or stunned.
