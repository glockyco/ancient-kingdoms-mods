Mechanics Reaper Form The form has no time limit. While the form is active, your magic damage gains 30%, and your damage-over-time ticks on other targets gain the same 30%. Lifetap healing grows with the damage it is based on. Each second, the form drains 5% of your maximum Health, rounded. With 2,000 maximum Health, you lose 100 Health each second. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Drain ignores resistance, wards and critical hits. The drain can kill you. It does not break Sleep. It ends when you toggle it off, remove it from your buff bar or mount. Death removes the form. The form persists when you log out and back in. The barber cannot change your appearance while the form is active. Using the skill again dismisses it immediately, even while feared or stunned.
