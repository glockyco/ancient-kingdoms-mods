Mechanics Reaper Form The form has no time limit. While the form is active, your Spell Power gains 30%, and your damage-over-time ticks on other targets gain the same 30%. Lifetap skills heal a share of the damage they deal, so magic lifetaps heal more while the form is active. Each second, the form drains 5% of your maximum Health, rounded. With 2,000 maximum Health, the form drains 100 Health each second. Your Health recovery still applies. The character window's Health regeneration shows the net change. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Drain ignores resistance, wards and critical hits. The drain can kill you. It does not break Sleep. It ends when you use the skill again (even while feared or stunned), remove it from your buff bar, mount, or die. The form persists when you log out and back in. The barber cannot change your appearance while the form is active.
