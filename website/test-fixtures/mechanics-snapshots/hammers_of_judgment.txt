Mechanics Skill Damage Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Cleric Rank multiplier 150% at rank 1, +25% per rank (375% at rank 10) Damage before defenses Magic Damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 10·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 750 damage before the target's defenses Rank multiplier: 150% + 25% × (10 − 1) = 375% Damage before defenses: 200 × 375% = 750, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns.
