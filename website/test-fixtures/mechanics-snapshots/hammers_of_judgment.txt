Mechanics Skill Damage Cleric Spell Power is the value in your character window. Damage % 150% at rank 1, +25% per rank (375% at rank 10) Damage before defenses Spell Power × Damage %, rounded Example ·,rank 10·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers 750 damage before the target's defenses Damage %: 150% + 25% × (10 − 1) = 375% Damage before defenses: 200 × 375% = 750, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
