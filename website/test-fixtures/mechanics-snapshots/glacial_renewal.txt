Mechanics Block Chance Raises the affected character's Block Chance by 50 percentage points at rank 1, to at most 80%. Block Chance applies only to physical hits. Block Chance is capped at 80% before Accuracy and level difference are applied. The final chance for an attack to miss is capped at 90%. Accuracy Raises the affected character's Accuracy by 25 percentage points at rank 1. The chance that its attacks and debuffs are blocked or resisted falls by the same number of percentage points, within each roll's limits. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
