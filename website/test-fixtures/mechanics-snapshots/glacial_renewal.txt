Mechanics Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Block Chance This skill adds its Block Chance bonus to the target's chance to block. Block Chance is capped at 80% before Accuracy and level difference are applied. The final chance for an attack to miss is capped at 90%. Accuracy This skill's Accuracy bonus lowers the target's chance to block or resist an attack by the same number of percentage points. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%.
