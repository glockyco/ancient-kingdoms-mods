Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Monsters marked Immune to Debuffs ignore this skill. Root Root stops movement and does not break when the target takes damage. Every 2 seconds, a rooted monster rolls to break free. Each point of its Magic Resist adds 0.1 percentage points to that chance, which stays between 5% and 95%. A rooted NPC has a 10% chance to break free every second. Bosses and elite monsters always resist this debuff. A rooted monster still attacks and casts at targets within its reach.
