Skill damage 2 Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage Example ·,the caster's 200 Damage 202 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 2 = 202 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
