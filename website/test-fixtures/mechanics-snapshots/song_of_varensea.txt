Mechanics Song Aura Song duration = base duration × (1 + learned song duration bonuses) Active Song Limit Active song limit = 2 + the bonus from Polyphony. A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Resist Chance clamp( target's Magic Resist × 0.0005 + clamp((target level − skill user level) × 0.005, −0.1, 0.1) − skill user's Accuracy , 0, 0.9) Every 100 points of the target's matching Resist add 5 percentage points to resist chance before level difference and Accuracy. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Sleep Sleep stops movement, but any direct damage or damage-over-time tick wakes the target. Bosses and elite monsters always resist sleep. Every 6 seconds, a sleeping monster has a chance to wake equal to its Magic Resist divided by 1,000, limited to 5–95%.
