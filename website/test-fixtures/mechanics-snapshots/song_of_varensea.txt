Mechanics Song Aura The song you select keeps recasting itself. Each cast takes 2.5s, shortened by Spell Casting Haste. Each completed cast renews the song for 20s. After you select another song, this one lasts the rest of its duration. Lingering Melody lengthens it by 10% per rank, so at rank 2 it lasts 24s. Active Song Limit A Bard sustains two songs by default (the Active Songs value in your character window). Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the song you renewed longest ago. See Bard Songs. Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Monsters marked Immune to Debuffs ignore this skill. Sleep Sleep stops movement and action, cancels a monster's cast and makes it drop its target. Direct damage, ordinary damage-over-time ticks and applying a damage-over-time debuff wake the target. Ordering your pet to attack a sleeping monster or NPC also wakes it. Reaper Form Health drain does not. Bosses and elite monsters always resist sleep. Every 6 seconds, a sleeping monster, NPC or pet rolls to wake. Each point of its Magic Resist adds 0.1 percentage points to that chance, which stays between 5% and 95%.
