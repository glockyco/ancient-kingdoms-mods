Mechanics Song Aura The song lasts 20s. Learned song-duration bonuses lengthen it by their total percentage, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 24s. Active Song Limit A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Reaper Form health drain does not. Bosses and elite monsters always resist sleep. Every 6 seconds, a sleeping monster rolls to wake.
