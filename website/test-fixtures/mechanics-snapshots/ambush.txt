Mechanics Skill Damage Rogue Damage is the value in your character window. Enter Base Damage from the weapon's tooltip, even if the weapon is broken. Enter 0 if no weapon is equipped in the named slot. Off-hand deduction Off-hand weapon's Base Damage × 50%, rounded up Skill damage 30 at rank 1, +30 per rank (150 at rank 5) Damage before defenses Damage + Skill damage − Off-hand deduction Example ·,rank 5·,200 Damage·,31 off-hand weapon Base Damage Use your numbers 334 damage before the target's defenses Off-hand deduction: 31 × 50% = 16, rounded up Skill damage: 30 + 30 × (5 − 1) = 150 Damage before defenses: 200 + 150 − 16 = 334 The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
