Mechanics Cleanse Mechanics Removes matching debuffs from you and every other living, visible player and pet within 10 yd, in your party or not. A cleanse removes only debuffs of the types it lists: poison, disease, cold, fire or magic. A debuff with none of those types, such as one that Armor Class resists, cannot be cleansed. The target cannot resist the cleanse. See cleanse calculations for layer removal and damage-over-time scaling.
