Mechanics When used by a mercenary, rank = floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10), capped at max rank. Debuff Scaling Attribute contribution = STR Defense reduction skill value at rank + attribute contribution × 0.4 Resist Chance clamp( target's Defense × 0.0005 + clamp((target level − skill user level) × 0.005, −0.1, 0.1) − skill user's Accuracy , 0, 0.9) Every 100 points of the target's Defense add 5 percentage points to resist chance before level difference and Accuracy. Cleanse Resistance A matching cleanse removes this debuff completely in a single cast. Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 0.6s cast into 0.48s.
