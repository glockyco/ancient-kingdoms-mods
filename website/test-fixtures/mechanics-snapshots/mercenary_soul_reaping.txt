Mechanics Companion skills gain a rank every 10 total veteran points (your VT), capped at the skill's maximum rank. Skill Damage Companion The caster uses its own Spell Power at its level. Skill damage 13 at rank 1, +3 per rank (100 at rank 30) Combined damage Spell Power + Skill damage Damage % 80% at rank 1, +0.6% per rank (97.4% at rank 30) Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,the caster's 200 Spell Power·,rank 1 170 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 13 + 3 × (1 − 1) = 13 Combined damage: 200 + 13 = 213 Damage %: 80% + 0.6% × (1 − 1) = 80% Damage before defenses: 213 × 80% = 170, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Cold Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals the attacker for 20% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 40. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap healing bonuses, such as Lich Shroud's, multiply the healing. See lifetap.
