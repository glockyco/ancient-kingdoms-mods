Fear Resist When fear would apply, the target has a chance to resist it. Total Fear Resist from all sources is capped at 100%. At 100%, the target resists every fear effect. Accuracy This skill's Accuracy bonus lowers the target's chance to block or resist an attack by the same number of percentage points. Total Accuracy is limited to a value between −50% and 100%. Critical Chance This skill adds to the attacker's critical hit chance, capped at 70%. Critical hits deal 1.5 times the damage of a noncritical hit.
