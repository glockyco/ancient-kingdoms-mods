Mechanics Scroll Mastery A scroll casts this skill at one rank per 5 percentage points of Scroll Mastery, rounded, from rank 1 up to rank 20. At 37% Scroll Mastery, it casts at rank 7. The Rank Scaling table lists the mastery each rank needs. Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. Resist Chance The target's Disease Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Once it lands, the target's Poison Resist reduces each tick by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. Because of a game defect, Poison Resist reduces this disease damage instead of Disease Resist. Cleanse layers and other tick modifiers also change the damage that lands. See damage-over-time calculations. This debuff has no effect on monsters whose pages list Immune to Debuffs.
