Mechanics Aggro Aggro = min(target health, skill aggro + caster maximum health + damage + round(stunChance × stunTime × 10) + round(fearChance × fearTime × 10) ) Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Fear Fear requires a non-fear-immune target that is not a boss monster, a successful fear roll and a failed fear resist roll, and no stun from the same hit. Fear lasts a random duration between half and all of the skill's fear duration. Fear applied by a Necromancer player halves a monster's or NPC's positive movement speed while feared.
