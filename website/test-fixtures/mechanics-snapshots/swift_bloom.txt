Mechanics When used by a mercenary, skill level = floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10), capped at max skill level. Buff Scaling Attribute contribution = WIS (mercenary's own attribute) Healing per second skill value at level × (1 + min(attribute contribution × 0.004, 5.0)) Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 1.2s cast into 0.96s.
