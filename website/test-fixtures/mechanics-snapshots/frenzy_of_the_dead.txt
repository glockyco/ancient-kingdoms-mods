Mechanics Companion Buff Targets your living companion within 12 yd. Familiars and mercenaries cannot receive this buff. Critical Resist Critical Resist reduces the extra damage from critical hits. See critical damage reduction for how the stat reduces the extra damage. At 100% Critical Resist, a critical hit deals the same damage as an ordinary hit. Each point of Dexterity above 0 adds 0.05 percentage points of Critical Resist. Gear and buffs can add more, up to a total of 100%.
