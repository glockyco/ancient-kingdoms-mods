Mechanics Healing Ranger Skill healing 750 at rank 1, +350 per rank (3,200 at rank 8) Wisdom percentage 1.2% per point of Wisdom above 0, at most 500% Wisdom healing Skill healing × Wisdom percentage, rounded Health restored Skill healing + Wisdom healing, at most the recipient's missing Health Example ·,rank 8·,100 Wisdom Use your numbers 7,040 Health before critical heals and the missing-Health cap Skill healing: 750 + 350 × (8 − 1) = 3,200 Wisdom percentage: 100 × 1.2% = 120% Wisdom healing: 3,200 × 120% = 3,840, rounded Health restored: 3,200 + 3,840 = 7,040 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. A heal is critical at the caster's Critical Chance. A critical heal restores double Health, and 1 in 10 critical heals restores triple, still capped by missing Health. Your Critical Chance is shown in the character window.
