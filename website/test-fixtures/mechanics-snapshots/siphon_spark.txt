Mechanics Skill Damage Necromancer Spell Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 15 at rank 1, +15 per rank (75 at rank 5) Combined damage Spell Power + Skill damage Damage % 65% at rank 1, +15% per rank (125% at rank 5) Damage before defenses Combined damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,rank 5·,200 Spell Power Use your numbers 344 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 15 + 15 × (5 − 1) = 75 Combined damage: 200 + 75 = 275 Damage %: 65% + 15% × (5 − 1) = 125% Damage before defenses: 275 × 125% = 344, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals the attacker for 50% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 100 at rank 5. Rank 1 gives 30%, and each rank adds 5%. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap healing bonuses, such as Lich Shroud's, multiply the healing. See lifetap.
