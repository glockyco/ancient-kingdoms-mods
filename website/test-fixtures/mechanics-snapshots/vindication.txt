Mechanics Skill Damage Damage is the value in your character window. Warrior Skill damage 1 Combined damage Damage + Skill damage Rank multiplier 110% at rank 1, +20% per rank (250% at rank 8) Damage before defenses Combined damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 8·,200 Damage Use your numbers 502 damage before the target's defenses Combined damage: 200 + 1 = 201 Rank multiplier: 110% + 20% × (8 − 1) = 250% Damage before defenses: 201 × 250% = 502, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Lifetap Heals you for 200% of each hit's damage after the target's defenses, Ward and Mana Shield, rounded down, so a 200-damage hit heals 400 at rank 8. Rank 1 gives 25%, and each rank adds 25%. Damage beyond the target's remaining Health still counts. Lifetap Healing bonuses multiply the healing. See lifetap.
