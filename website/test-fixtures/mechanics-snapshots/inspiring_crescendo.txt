Mechanics Song Aura Song duration = base duration × (1 + learned song duration bonuses) Active Song Limit Active song limit = 2 + the bonus from Polyphony. A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Song Scaling Song multiplier = 1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2) final value = base value at skill level × song multiplier Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste) Critical Chance This skill adds to the attacker's critical hit chance, capped at 70%. Critical hits deal 1.5 times the damage of a noncritical hit.
