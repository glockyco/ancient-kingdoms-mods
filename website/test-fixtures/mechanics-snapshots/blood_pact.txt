Mechanics Blood Pact Conversion percentage 5% at skill level 1, +5% per level (25% at level 5)Health spent conversion percentage of your maximum Health, rounded upMana restored conversion percentage of your maximum Mana, rounded down, at most your missing Mana Example ·,skill level 1·,2,000 maximum Health·,1,500 maximum Mana·,600 missing Mana Spends 100 Health and restores 75 Mana Health spent: 5% of 2,000 = 100Mana restored: 5% of 1,500 = 75 You can use it only while your Mana is below maximum, your current Health is above the Health spent and both amounts are above 0. You pay the full Health cost even when the Mana restored is capped. Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 0.5s cast into 0.4s.
