Mechanics Skill Damage Warrior Attack Power is the value in your character window. Damage % 125% at rank 1, +25% per rank (300% at rank 8) Damage before defenses Attack Power × Damage %, rounded Example ·,rank 8·,200 Attack Power Use your numbers 600 damage before the target's defenses Damage %: 125% + 25% × (8 − 1) = 300% Damage before defenses: 200 × 300% = 600, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
