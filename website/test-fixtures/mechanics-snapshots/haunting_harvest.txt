Mechanics The target resists this debuff using Magic Resist. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has three layers, and cleansing it removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each extra-layer roll starts at 75%. Accuracy increases that chance. See cleanse calculations. Root Root stops movement and does not break when the target takes damage. Every 2 seconds, a rooted monster rolls to break free. Each point of its Magic Resist adds 0.1 percentage points to that chance, which stays between 5% and 95%. A rooted NPC has a 10% chance to break free every second. Bosses and elite monsters always resist this debuff.
