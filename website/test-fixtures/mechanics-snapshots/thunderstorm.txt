Mechanics Skill Damage Druid Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Skill damage 80 at rank 1, +40 per rank (280 at rank 6) Damage before defenses Magic Damage + Skill damage Example ·,rank 6·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 480 damage before the target's defenses Skill damage: 80 + 40 × (6 − 1) = 280 Damage before defenses: 200 + 280 = 480 The target's Magic Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short.
