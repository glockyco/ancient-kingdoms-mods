Mechanics Healing Druid Skill healing 1,500 at rank 1, +400 per rank (3,500 at rank 6) Wisdom percentage 0.4% per point of Wisdom above 0, at most 500% Wisdom healing Skill healing × Wisdom percentage, rounded Health restored Skill healing + Wisdom healing, at most the recipient's missing Health Example ·,rank 6·,100 Wisdom Use your numbers 4,900 Health before critical heals and the missing-Health cap Skill healing: 1,500 + 400 × (6 − 1) = 3,500 Wisdom percentage: 100 × 0.4% = 40% Wisdom healing: 3,500 × 40% = 1,400, rounded Health restored: 3,500 + 1,400 = 4,900 Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. A heal is critical at the caster's Critical Chance. A critical heal restores double Health, and 1 in 10 critical heals restores triple, still capped by missing Health. Your Critical Chance is shown in the character window.
