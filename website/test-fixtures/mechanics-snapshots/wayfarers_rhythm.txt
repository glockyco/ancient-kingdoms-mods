Mechanics Song Aura The song lasts 20s. Learned song-duration bonuses lengthen it by their total percentage, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 24s. Active Song Limit A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Song Category: Bard Travel Song Song strength adds its movement speed, physical damage percentage, magic damage percentage, Haste, spell haste and Accuracy bonuses. A target keeps one song of each category, including songs from other Bards and Bard mercenaries. A stronger song replaces a song in the same category. At equal strength, the song already active stays.
