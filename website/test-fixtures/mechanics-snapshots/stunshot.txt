Mechanics Level contribution Your level ÷ 5, rounded down Veteran contribution Total veteran points ÷ 10, rounded down Skill rank Level contribution + veteran contribution, at most the skill's maximum rank Total veteran points are your unspent veteran points plus the ranks of your learned veteran skills. A game defect counts a veteran skill rank that costs 2 points as 1 point. Each such rank lowers your Veteran Level by 1 and the next point's experience cost by 20,000 XP, and a token reset refunds only 1 of its 2 points. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%. See the damage pipeline, resistance and avoidance for level differences and Accuracy, and moving targets and backstabs. Stun Each hit rolls once against the stun chance. A target that is already feared cannot be stunned. A stun on a target that is already stunned ends at whichever end time is later, so a shorter stun never cuts an existing one short. A stunned player is dismounted. A player riding a Bear Mount resists 90% of stuns.
