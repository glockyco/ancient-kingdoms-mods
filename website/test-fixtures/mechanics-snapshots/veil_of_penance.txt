Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Once it lands, the target's Magic Resist reduces each tick by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. Cleanse layers and other tick modifiers also change the damage that lands. See damage-over-time calculations. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has 50% Cleanse Resist and three layers. Cleansing removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each of those rolls succeeds at 50% × (100% + the cleansing caster's Accuracy), limited to 0–100%. At 100%, one cast removes all three layers. See cleanse calculations. After a cleanse removes layers, each damage tick deals 85% with two layers left and 70% with one, rounded up. Blindness Blindness covers the affected player's screen with a black overlay for the effect's duration. It changes no combat stats and affects only players. The overlay fades out over 1 second when the effect ends.
