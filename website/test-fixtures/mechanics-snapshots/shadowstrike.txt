Mechanics Skill Damage Rogue Attack Power is the value in your character window. Enter Base Damage from the weapon's tooltip, even if the weapon is broken. Enter 0 if no weapon is equipped in the named slot. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
