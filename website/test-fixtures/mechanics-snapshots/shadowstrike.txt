Rogue Off-hand deduction Off-hand weapon damage × 50%, rounded up Skill damage 1 Combined damage Damage + Skill damage − Off-hand deduction Rank multiplier 200% at rank 1, +60% per rank (500% at rank 6) Damage before defenses Combined damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 6·,200 Damage·,31 off-hand weapon damage Use your numbers 925 damage before the target's defenses Off-hand deduction: 31 × 50% = 16, rounded up Combined damage: 200 + 1 − 16 = 185 Rank multiplier: 200% + 60% × (6 − 1) = 500% Damage before defenses: 185 × 500% = 925, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Defense reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Defense and any block bonuses, at most 80%.
