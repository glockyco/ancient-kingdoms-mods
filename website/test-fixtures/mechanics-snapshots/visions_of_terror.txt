Mechanics Fear Monsters and companions whose pages list Immune to Fear cannot be feared. Every boss is immune. A hit rolls against the fear chance only when it does not stun: the skill has no stun chance, its stun roll fails, or the target is already feared. If the fear roll succeeds, the target rolls its Fear Resist and is feared only if that roll fails. Fear lasts a random time from 3s to 6s at rank 1. A new fear replaces the remaining fear time, so it can end sooner. A feared player is dismounted. A monster or NPC feared by a Necromancer player moves at half its normal speed while feared.
