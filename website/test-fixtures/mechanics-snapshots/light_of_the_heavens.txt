Mechanics When used by a mercenary, rank = floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10), capped at max rank. Healing Formula Final Heal = Base Heal + round(Base Heal × min(WIS × 0.004, 5.0)) Mercenaries use their own Wisdom to increase healing. Ranger mercenaries do not receive the triple-Wisdom bonus that Ranger players receive. Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 1.8s cast into 1.44s.
