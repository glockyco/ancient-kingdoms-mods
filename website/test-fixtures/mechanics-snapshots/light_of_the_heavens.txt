Mechanics When used by a mercenary, skill level = floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10), capped at max skill level. Healing Formula Final Heal = Base Heal + round(Base Heal × min(WIS × 0.004, 5.0)) Mercenaries use their own Wisdom to increase healing. Ranger mercenaries do not receive the triple-Wisdom bonus that Ranger players receive. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
