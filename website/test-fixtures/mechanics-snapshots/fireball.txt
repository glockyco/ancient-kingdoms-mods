Mechanics Skill Damage Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Druid Rank multiplier 200% at rank 1, +30% per rank (350% at rank 6) Damage before defenses Magic Damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 6·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 700 damage before the target's defenses Rank multiplier: 200% + 30% × (6 − 1) = 350% Damage before defenses: 200 × 350% = 700, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Fire Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely.
