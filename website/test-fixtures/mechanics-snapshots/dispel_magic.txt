Mechanics The target resists this debuff using Magic Resist. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Dispel Mechanics You cast this on a monster to remove its beneficial buffs. First the monster can resist the skill, then each buff has a separate removal chance. 1. Resist. Your Accuracy lowers the monster's chance to resist this skill (shown above). If the monster resists, no buffs are removed. 2. Remove buffs. Each buff has a separate removal roll. The chance to remove a buff is 100% minus its Dispel Resist, plus your Dispel Resist reduction. A buff with 0% Dispel Resist is always removed. Half your Accuracy reduces Dispel Resist by the same number of percentage points. Your Accuracy helps the dispel land and reduces each buff's Dispel Resist. For example, 20% Accuracy lowers Dispel Resist by 10 percentage points.
