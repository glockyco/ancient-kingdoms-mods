Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Magic Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this skill, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. This skill has no effect on monsters whose pages list Immune to Debuffs. Dispel Mechanics You cast this on a monster to remove its beneficial buffs. First the monster can resist the skill, then each buff has a separate removal chance. 1. Resist. The monster rolls to resist this skill with its Magic Resist, and your Accuracy lowers the chance. If the monster resists, no buffs are removed. 2. Remove buffs. Each buff has a separate removal roll. The chance to remove a buff is 100% minus its Cleanse Resist (listed in the buff's Effect row), plus your Cleanse Resist reduction, limited to 0–100%. A buff with 0% Cleanse Resist is always removed unless your Accuracy is negative. Monster buffs with Cleanse Resist: Ember Renewal (100%), Fabled Blessing of Nogroth (100%), Blazeguard (75%), Blessing of Seraphax (75%), Emberguard (60%), Blessing of Forest (50%), Blood Fury (50%), Bloodrage Command (50%), Dragon Shield (50%), Flameveil (50%), Frostcrown's Wrath (50%), Northgate's Frenzy (50%), Primal Fury (30%), Frostheart Renewal (25%), Tides of Restoration (20%). Every other monster buff has 0%. Your reduction is half your Accuracy: 20% Accuracy lowers each buff's Cleanse Resist by 10 percentage points. Negative Accuracy raises it.
