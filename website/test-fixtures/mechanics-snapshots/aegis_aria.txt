Mechanics Song Aura The song you select keeps recasting itself. Each cast takes 3.5s, shortened by Spell Casting Haste. Each completed cast renews the song for 12s. After you select another song, this one lasts the rest of its duration. A learned song-duration bonus such as Lingering Melody lengthens it, so with a 20% bonus it lasts 14.4s. Active Song Limit A Bard sustains two songs by default (the Active Songs value in your character window). Starting another song at the limit ends the song you renewed longest ago. Learning Polyphony removes the limit. See Bard Songs. Song Category: Ward Ward capacity does not count toward song strength, so this song's strength is 0. At equal strength, the song already active stays. Ward is also an overwrite group for other buffs. This song replaces a non-song Ward buff, and another Ward buff can replace this song, regardless of ward capacity. See Bard Songs.
