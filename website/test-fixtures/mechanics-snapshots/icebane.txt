Mechanics Resist Chance The target's Cold Resist gives it 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist this debuff, at most 90%. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has 60% Cleanse Resist and three layers. Cleansing removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each of those rolls succeeds at 40% × (100% + the cleansing caster's Accuracy), limited to 0–100%. At 100%, one cast removes all three layers. See cleanse calculations. After a cleanse removes layers, each damage tick deals 85% with two layers left and 70% with one, rounded up.
