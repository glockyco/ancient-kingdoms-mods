Mechanics Skill Damage Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Wizard Rank multiplier 150% at rank 1, +35% per rank (325% at rank 6)Damage before defenses Magic Damage × Rank multiplier, rounded Example ·,rank 6·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 650 damage before the target's defenses Rank multiplier: 150% + 35% × (6 − 1) = 325%Damage before defenses: 200 × 325% = 650, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Cold Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely.
