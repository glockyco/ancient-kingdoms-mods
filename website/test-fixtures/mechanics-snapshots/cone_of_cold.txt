Mechanics Skill Damage Wizard Magic Damage is the value in your character window. Damage % 150% at rank 1, +35% per rank (325% at rank 6) Damage before defenses Magic Damage × Damage %, rounded Example ·,rank 6·,200 Magic Damage Use your numbers 650 damage before the target's defenses Damage %: 150% + 35% × (6 − 1) = 325% Damage before defenses: 200 × 325% = 650, rounded Rounded means to the nearest whole number. Exact halves go to the even number, so 2.5 becomes 2. The target's Cold Resist reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up, and gives 5 percentage points of chance per 100 points to resist the hit entirely. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
