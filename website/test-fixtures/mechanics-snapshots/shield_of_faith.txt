Mechanics Mercenary Skill Rank A mercenary uses this skill at a rank set by your level and total veteran points. See Mercenary Skill Ranks. Level contribution Your level ÷ 5, rounded down Veteran contribution Veteran points (VT) ÷ 10, rounded down Skill rank Level contribution + veteran contribution, at most the skill's maximum rank Total veteran points (Veteran Level) are your unspent veteran points plus your learned veteran skill ranks. The character window shows them as the VT number beside your level, as in "Level 50 (VT 20)". Use 0 if no VT number appears.
