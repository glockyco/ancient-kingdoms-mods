Mechanics When used by a mercenary, skill level = floor(regular level ÷ 5) + floor(veteran level ÷ 10), capped at max skill level. Buff Scaling Attribute contribution = WIS (mercenary's own attribute) Defense skill value at level + attribute contribution × 0.15 Spell haste shortens the cast by its percentage, so 20% spell haste turns the 2.1s cast into 1.68s.
