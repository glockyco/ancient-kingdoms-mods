Mechanics Skill Damage Warrior Attack Power is the value in your character window. Skill damage 1 Damage before defenses Attack Power + Skill damage Example ·,200 Attack Power Use your numbers 201 damage before the target's defenses Damage before defenses: 200 + 1 = 201 The target's Armor Class reduces this damage by 5% per 100 points, at most 90%, with the reduction rounded up. The hit can also be blocked or missed, at the target's base block chance plus 1 percentage point per 100 Armor Class and any block bonuses, at most 80%. Level differences and Accuracy change these chances: see resistance and avoidance. See also the damage pipeline and moving targets and backstabs.
