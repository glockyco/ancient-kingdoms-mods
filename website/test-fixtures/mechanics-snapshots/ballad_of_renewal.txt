Mechanics Song Aura Song duration = base duration × (1 + learned song duration bonuses) Active Song Limit Active song limit = 2 + the bonus from Polyphony. A Bard can sustain two songs by default. Learning Polyphony raises the limit to three. Starting another song at the limit ends the oldest active song. Song Scaling Song multiplier = 1 + min(max(CHA, 0) × 0.001, 2) final value = round(base value at skill level × song multiplier) Each healing tick can critically heal for 1.5× at the Bard's critical chance. Cast time = cast time × (1 − spell haste)
