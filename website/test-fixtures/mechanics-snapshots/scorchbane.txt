Mechanics Debuff Scaling Monster or NPC This debuff receives no attribute bonus. The target resists this debuff using Fire Resist. See resistance and avoidance for Accuracy and level differences. Cleanse Resistance This debuff has three layers, and cleansing it removes the debuff only after all three are gone. A matching cleanse always removes one layer. It rolls twice more to remove one additional layer per roll. Each extra-layer roll starts at 50%. Accuracy increases that chance. See cleanse calculations. While layers remain, each damage tick deals 85% of its full damage at two layers or 70% at one layer.
