Profession pages — design spikes

Three prototypes against the thesis form follows the shape of the fact. Every number is real, queried from compendium.db or derived from the cited server script. Left is what ships today; right is the proposal.

Spike A

The tier grid becomes a curve

Mining success against skill, for all five ore tiers. The grid states five numbers for one slider position. The curve states the whole function, marks where you are, and shades the regions where a tier stops paying skill — a rule no page currently states at all. Formulas: Utils.cs:GetSuccessProbMining, GatherItem.cs:OnInteractServer.

42%
2
Ships today

Five percentages for one slider position. Nothing shows the shape of the curve, where a tier becomes reliable, or where it stops granting skill. To learn any of that you drag the slider and watch numbers change.

Proposed

What it buys. The shaded band is the "too simple" rule: above 25% skill tier I stops granting mastery, above 50% tiers I–II stop, above 75% tiers I–III stop. That is the single most useful progression fact in the game and it is currently unstated on every one of the eight calculator pages. As an encoding it costs less vertical space than the grid it replaces.

What it buys. The metric strip was a table of contents that forgot to be clickable. Turning it into one keeps every count, adds wayfinding to a page that is 12.5 mobile screens with eight sections and no navigation, and reclaims roughly 2.5 screens. The payoff sentence is the fact a reader actually arrived for, and no profession page states it today.

Radiant Seeker

Gather Radiant Sparks for Radiant Aether, a combat consumable. Aether turns a critical hit from ×1.5 into ×3, saves you from a killing blow, and can cancel an area attack outright. Skill raises how often a spark yields one, from 5% to 25%.
